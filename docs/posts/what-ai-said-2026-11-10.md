# What AI Said this week: one question, five engines, and what a single check misses

> **Operator disclosure.** Broadcastwell ran these measurements and sells services in the category it measures. Broadcastwell is not measured in its own Index.

## What moved this week

Nothing moved in the Absence Index this week. Release 2026-09, published 23 September 2026, remains current. It covers 14 categories, 286 checked vendors and 2,095 valid answers across five engines: ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. Each category has ten fixed buyer questions, each run three times on each engine. Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode are recorded separately because they are two products.

## How to read these numbers

A count in the Index always comes with its base. Named in 109 of 150 answers means 109 of the 150 valid answers for that category named the vendor at least once. Where we give a range, it is a 95 percent interval on that count. When two vendors' intervals overlap, the order between them is not established by these data, so we do not call one ahead of the other.

A zero is a measured absence in this release. It says those answers on those dates did not name the vendor. It does not say anything about the vendor's product, and it says nothing about other dates. Our repeat-run study measures variation within a single day, so what happens over weeks remains unmeasured here.

Third-party figures are quoted as their authors state them. Ahrefs notes that sources shift between generations, so the overlap it measured may understate the true overlap. SparkToro's figures describe how often the same list or order came back across many repeated runs. Semrush's ghost citation figure is a share of domain appearances in its dataset, not a share of all citations in AI answers.
