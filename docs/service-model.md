# Which workstreams make up the Broadcastwell service model?

The Broadcastwell service model has six defined workstreams: question and scope, measurement, source and record work, page repair and build, re-check and reporting, and ChatGPT Business Hub readiness. Every offer draws on some of them, and none adds a workstream you did not buy. This page names each workstream's output, its owner and the offers that include it, as published on 9 November 2026.

Updated 9 November 2026.

Our [operating framework](operating-framework.md) sets out the order of the work and quotes every delivery clock and refund term. This page answers a different question: who does what, and in which offer.

## Workstream 1: question and scope

**Output:** the agreed buyer questions, the scope, the country and a named approver on your side. For Answer Repair, recorded Ready. For a Fix Sprint, one primary test agreed before payment: naming, own-domain citation or buyer-task completion.

**Owner:** our team proposes and records; your named approver confirms. "Each client's agreed question set stays private," in the words of our method page.

**Country:** "One chosen country, United States by default; declared location and network country reported separately." Care includes one country; Program includes up to three a month.

## Workstream 2: measurement

**Output:** dated answers from ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode, with every cited URL, valid answers and exclusions stated separately, and a receipt for each answer.

**Owner:** our engineers run the capture under method v1.1; every finding is checked against its receipts before delivery.

| Offer | Measurement included |
| --- | --- |
| Live question | 15 planned answers on one question |
| Answer Repair | A five-answer question record, one answer per engine, at Ready |
| Agency Deadline Audit | 150 planned answers on ten agreed questions |
| Fix Sprint | Day 0 and day 30 evidence on the agreed test |
| Care | Monthly evidence on the agreed scope |
| Program | Monthly evidence and a separate measurement report |

## Workstream 3: source and record work

**Output:** where the answers come from and what they say about you. The published evidence sections are the source breakdown, the ranked lists that answer your question, the competitor pages shaping your answers, the Record Check with fidelity and the public footprint record. In Care and Program this workstream also changes outside sources: corrections, listings, profile claims and quotes offered to editors, each reported with its receipt.

**Owner:** our consultants classify and act; statements about you are checked against facts you approved.

## Workstream 4: page repair and build

**Output:** pages on your own site. Answer Repair delivers one source-backed page or fix, Live or as a Handoff, with approved facts, a before-and-after diff, a release receipt and a CEO note. A Fix Sprint delivers six contracted pages or rewrites over 30 days. Program delivers twelve pages or rewrites and 8 channel posts a month. You own the domain, content and exports.

**Owner:** our team writes and builds; your named approver checks the facts before anything goes live.

## Workstream 5: re-check and reporting

**Output:** the same questions measured again under the same method version, reported count by count with its base. The Fix Sprint reports the day-30 test as pass, no-pass or coverage-failed, with refund status. The Fix Sprint also includes the seven-part Visibility Vector at day 30, Manipulation Watch at day 0 and measurement setup at onboarding. Program adds monthly reporting.

**Owner:** our engineers measure; Priya Nair, Management Consultant, is the delivery lead and reviewer.

The [completed-case format](cases/format.md) shows every field a finished re-check reports, on the fictional Kalvenor sample.

## Workstream 6: ChatGPT Business Hub readiness

**Output:** six readiness parts for OpenAI's reported, unreleased ChatGPT Business Hub, set out in our [Business Hub guide](chatgpt-business-hub/index.md). The Fix Sprint includes parts 1 to 4; Care and Program include all six.

## What no workstream includes

No workstream makes an engine name or cite you, and none reports a composite score. We never plant hidden text, fake reviews or instructions to models, and we do not contact your clients.
