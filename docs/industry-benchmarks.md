# What do our AI answer industry benchmarks show?

Our AI answer industry benchmarks set fourteen B2B software categories side by side from one dated sample: 286 checked vendors, 2,095 valid answers captured on 22 and 23 September 2026, ten fixed buyer questions per category and three runs on each of five engines. Naming concentration ran from 30.8% in transportation management to 67.0% in CRM.

Updated 9 November 2026.

The five engines are ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. Every figure below is published in the September release of the Absence Index or in the source tables of our report on it, with its count and base. The question bank and the checked vendor set are fixed, not a probability sample of buyers or vendors, and the repeats can be dependent. Read a row as what the engines answered to those questions on those dates.

The top three share is the three most-named vendors' names over all checked-vendor names in that category, from our [vendor concentration table](research/data/vendor-concentration.csv); an answer can name several vendors. The vendor-site share uses the post-audit source classes over every cited entry. We do not pool the most-named vendor across categories, because each category has its own checked set and its own questions.

## What the spread tells a buyer

**Measured.** Vendor sites carried 49.0% of citations in property management software answers and 21.7% in dealership software. The most answers without any cited entry came in field service software: 9 of 150. Five target attempts were excluded across all categories, every one a Google AI Overviews attempt, and excluded attempts leave both the count and the base.

**Reasoned.** A category where a few vendors take most of the names is harder to enter from outside that group, and a category where vendor sites carry most citations puts more weight on a vendor's own pages. Neither pattern says why an engine answered as it did, and neither predicts the next capture. Our registered persistence study waits for comparable releases before comparing standings over time; see [does low presence persist](research/studies/does-absence-persist/index.md).
