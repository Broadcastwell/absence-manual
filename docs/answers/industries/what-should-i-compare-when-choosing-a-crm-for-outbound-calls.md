# What should I compare when choosing a CRM for outbound calls?

Our study, [The AI written RFP](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23271935) (request for proposal), puts the call record and its handoff at the center of this comparison: the 22 September 2026 CRM (customer relationship management) panel states activity tracking in 18 of its 150 answers. Compare how a call's outcome and next step remain attached to the customer, rather than treating a vendor naming total as a calling-product test.

Observed answers on stated dates, not a ranking.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [study-specific coding method](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23271935) identifies requirements stated before the first vendor mention. Fifteen activity-tracking records come from the handoff question, one per engine and repeat; three more belong to the CRM evaluation question. The published 95% interval is 7.7% to 18.2%. Team review: in progress. These observations did not test a dialer, caller privacy settings or live outbound calls. The [original naming collection](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) provides all ten checked suppliers and their thirty-answer engine bases below.

One [ChatGPT answer](https://zenodo.org/records/22907695/files/answers-2026-09.jsonl?download=1) captured on 22 September 2026 includes this wording:

> Log meaningful call and meeting outcomes, then use that context to summarize the customer’s priorities

| Copper | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 3 |
| Freshsales | 0 | 8 | 9 | 2 | 3 |
| HubSpot CRM | 24 | 24 | 22 | 20 | 23 |

| Zoho CRM | 15 | 18 | 12 | 13 | 15 |

**Reasoned.** A requirement to retain call history gives an outbound team a particular item to compare. It describes continuity between people using a customer record; it does not establish calling speed, recording permission or a vendor's telephony entitlement.

## What this means for you

Follow the completed conversation. In the first ChatGPT return for the [handoff question](https://index.broadcastwell.com/questions/crm/how-can-a-sales-team-use-a-crm-to-improve-handoffs-and-follow/), the retained passage asks for meaningful call and meeting outcomes, customer priorities and the agreed next step. The third return places calls, emails, notes and commitments against the customer and deal. Those particular fields are useful comparison items when the next representative needs to know what the prospect already said.

The second ChatGPT return adds another kind of handover detail: customer goals, key contacts, qualification information, objections and commitments. A status label alone does not contain those fields. For your outbound process, compare the record that the receiving person actually sees with that source passage, rather than counting the presence of a call button as evidence that the handoff context is retained.

The evaluation question contributes a separate contact-profile requirement. Its second Overview return describes emails, calls, notes and previous interactions in one profile. That is a comparison of the record surrounding a conversation. A provider's claims about placing or recording calls would need their own account, feature and permission source; they are not supplied by the eighteen answers stating an activity requirement.
