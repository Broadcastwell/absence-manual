# Why is my radiology software missing from AI answers?

The question about what to look for in a new radiology PACS named none of the 24 checked vendors across fifteen valid answers captured on 22 September 2026. Across the full 150-answer radiology category, only Canon Medical Informatics and Hyland were never named. That difference makes the question essential to any absence report. The collection does not identify why a supplier was omitted.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [September radiology and medical-imaging panel](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) contains ten fixed questions with three scheduled answers on ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. All 150 observations were valid. The table shows the complete category-wide zero set, rather than the larger set left unnamed on the PACS-criteria question.

Source lists are a separate observation. reddit.com appeared in 60 of 150 category answer lists and intelerad.com in forty. Those pooled host counts can help locate material for inspection, but neither one establishes the cause of a missing name or verifies a PACS function.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Preserve the setting behind your concern: an outpatient centre, a group of imaging locations or a hospital team. A question asking for selection criteria should not inherit the same expectation of product names as an open product search. Find the actual retained question before classifying the observation.

Check the vendor identity and aliases used in the record. A checked zero and an unchecked company are different states. If the product was outside the twenty-four-vendor roster, this collection supplies no measurement for it. Do not turn that missing coverage into a zero.

For an error investigation, retain one claim about the named system and the dated source needed to check it. Resolve the exact product or edition before proposing a factual correction. These historical omissions do not demonstrate a product defect, establish system suitability or predict a naming change after a page edit.
