# Why is my loan origination software missing from AI answers?

Keep the lender or broker in the question when investigating a missing loan-origination name. Four of 25 checked vendors were never named across 150 valid answers captured on 22 September 2026. On the small-mortgage-broker question, 22 checked vendors had no names across its fifteen answers. These are observed gaps, without evidence of their cause or of a defect in the omitted software.

## What we measured

**Measured.** We summed the vendor-name observations in the [September loan-origination release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695). It contains ten fixed questions observed three times each on ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. All 150 scheduled answers were valid. The alphabetical table contains every vendor with no matches across that whole category.

Each zero-of-150 result has a descriptive Wilson 95 percent interval of 0.0 to 2.5 percent. Publication was on 23 September 2026, after the 22 September captures. A zero follows the stated vendor and alias matching rules; it is not an assessment of lending functions or implementation results.

For the small-broker question, the three checked names that appeared were Calyx, ICE Mortgage Technology and LendingPad. Their counts were thirteen, six and fifteen of fifteen valid answers respectively. This question-level subset sits inside the 150-answer category; it is not another independent collection to add to the total.

The broader panel also asks about community banks, credit unions and in-house lending. A category result mixes these settings. The 22 question-level omissions should not be relabelled as vendors that were absent throughout the category.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Write down the institution and task that prompted your concern before opening a broad visibility report. A broker-shop question and a community-bank question should retain their own wording. Read the nearest recorded question and its answer before deciding which product claim needs inspection.

Establish whether your supplier was on the checked roster. If it was, review the recorded name and aliases against the answer. If it was not, describe the result as unmeasured. Assigning zero to an unchecked company would manufacture an observation the panel never made.

If an answer misstates a verifiable product fact, preserve that sentence with dated public evidence. The category source lists can help locate a page to examine, but a missing name does not identify a source defect. A website edit and a later AI answer require separate records before anyone can assess what changed.
