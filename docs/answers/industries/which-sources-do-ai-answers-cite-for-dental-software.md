# Which sources do AI answers cite for dental software?

The dental source lists included medixdental.com in 47 of 149 valid answers and thedentalapp.com in 44, captured on 22 September 2026. The record contains 136 distinct domains, including vendor sites and other publishers. That is evidence of which hosts appeared in this collection, not a finding that every page on those hosts supports a dental-software claim.

## What we measured

**Measured.** Our [September Index release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) retained 149 valid dental answers from ten questions observed three times on ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. Of 150 scheduled observations, one returned no Google AI Overview and was excluded. The following examples are ordered by domain name, with a host counted once per valid answer.

medixdental.com's 47 of 149 is 31.5 percent; its descriptive 95 percent Wilson interval is 24.6 to 39.4 percent. The domain list is pooled across the engines and was published on 23 September 2026. It does not establish an engine-specific citation rate or the influence of one source on a returned name.

Several domains can appear in one answer, so these rows overlap. They do not divide dental research traffic into shares, count buyers or identify the number of pages used on each website.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Begin with the answer's particular claim. A comparison of Dentrix and Eaglesoft, advice for a very small office and a multi-location DSO question have different scopes. Look for the actual cited page attached to the answer you are inspecting, rather than borrowing the category's most frequent domain as a substitute receipt.

Check whether the passage concerns the same product, edition and date. A source may describe a general practice-management category while the answer makes a narrower statement about an integration or a commercial condition. Keep those levels apart. If the supporting passage is missing or more limited, mark the claim unresolved until suitable evidence is available.

For your own dental-software page, make the supported fact easy to locate and keep its date visible. This record does not show that copying the style of a cited publisher will secure a future citation. Nor does a domain's frequent appearance validate its clinical or operational advice. Use the source list to locate something you can read, then decide whether that particular statement is supported.
