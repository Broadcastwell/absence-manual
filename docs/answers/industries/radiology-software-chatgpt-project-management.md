# What can radiology software with ChatGPT do for project management?

On 13 April 2023, RamSoft announced ChatGPT-powered imaging-procedure explanations in Blume; that source does not document project-management operations. Our [radiology criterion study](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23271935) found a scheduling criterion in 2 of 149 radiology answers captured on 22 September 2026. The requirement helps separate appointment work from the project-planning claim in this question.

Observed answers on stated dates, not a ranking.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [study-specific coding method](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23271935) codes buyer requirements before the first vendor mention. Two Google AI Mode records from the outpatient radiology information system (RIS) question state efficient patient and examination scheduling, in its first and third returns. Its denominator is 149 after a later off-topic exclusion; the [original Index](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) below retains all 150 original radiology returns and thirty per engine, dated 22 September 2026. **Reported.** The [RamSoft launch announcement](https://www.ramsoft.com/press-releases/industry-first-medical-imaging-patient-app-blume-powered-by-chatgpt?hs_amp=true), saved on 29 October 2026, describes patient questions about magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), ultrasound and positron emission tomography/computed tomography (PET/CT) procedures. It lists appointment requests, intake forms and image/report sharing as other Blume functions, and names a demonstration with OmegaAI at HIMSS 2023.

**Reasoned.** A requirement for examination scheduling and a patient-app appointment function concern a specific clinical event. Their presence does not establish that the announced chat feature creates a project plan or operates those other app functions.

## What this means for you

Name the requested operation. In RamSoft's announcement, the chat feature answers a patient's procedure question in plain language. If your intended project is a picture archiving and communication system (PACS) replacement, that passage supplies no migration schedule, staff assignment or task-dependency operation. Those are different requests from explaining what an ultrasound examination involves.

Blume's appointment wording is specific too: the app supports self-scheduling and managing appointment requests. Intake submission and sharing medical images appear as separate activities in the same list. The announcement does not assign every item in that list to the ChatGPT-powered explanation feature.
