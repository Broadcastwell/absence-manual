# Which sources does AI cite for property management software?

buildium.com appeared as a cited host in 75 of 148 valid property-management answers captured on 22 September 2026; doorloop.com appeared in 47 and appfolio.com in 38. A landlord reading those citations still needs the exact page behind the software claim. The counts pool five engines and do not establish which product fits a rental portfolio.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [September release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) used ten property-management questions, each repeated three times on ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. Two scheduled Overview observations returned no AI Overview; the valid base is 148. A host counts once per answer. buildium.com's 75 of 148 is 50.7 percent, with a descriptive 95 percent Wilson interval of 42.7 to 58.6 percent. Answers were captured on 22 September 2026 and published on 23 September.

These six selected hosts are listed alphabetically from an inventory of 158; the table does not show every source. Naming and citing remain different events: the checked name Buildium appeared in 119 of 148 answers, while its host appeared in 75. The two omitted observations were on the Buildium-versus-AppFolio and software-evaluation-criteria questions. Neither is a valid answer with an empty citation list.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Use the source to answer a portfolio-specific question. For a fifty-unit landlord, choose the corresponding saved answer rather than importing a conclusion from the growing-management-company question. Follow its citation and note whether the passage covers the particular operation you are evaluating.

For a statement about a rent record, maintenance handoff or report, preserve the stated product and any plan condition. Those are examples of requirements to check, not capabilities established by this study. If a comparison gives no source for the detail you need, leave that requirement unknown until a readable primary document settles it.

Keep the destination's role visible in your notes. A product page, a review directory and a supplier comparison may support different kinds of statements. Copy the relevant passage with its date rather than recording only the company domain. A reader should be able to see how far the source supports the claim without repeating your entire search.

If you maintain a property-software page, correct one supported mismatch at a time and read the served wording after the edit. Do not treat the host count as a reason to buy placement or as evidence that every page on that host was used. The collection measured citations, without testing the effect of an edit, a listing or a commercial arrangement.
