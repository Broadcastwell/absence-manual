# Which sources do AI answers cite for CRM software?

Pipedrive and Pipeline CRM are different host entries in the CRM citation record. In answers captured on 22 September 2026, pipedrive.com appeared in 43 of 150 CRM answers and pipelinecrm.com in 32; salesforce.com appeared in 33. The counts pool five engines and count a host once within each answer. They tell you where to inspect evidence, without establishing that the source endorsed a product or settled the answer's claim.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [September Index](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) used ten CRM questions with three runs each on ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. It retained 150 valid answers. pipedrive.com's 43 of 150 is 28.7 percent, with a descriptive 95 percent Wilson interval of 22.0 to 36.4 percent. Answers were captured on 22 September 2026 and the release was published on 23 September.

The table gives six selected examples, alphabetically, from 265 recorded hosts. It does not show every source. A vendor's name and a cited publisher are different fields: HubSpot CRM was named in 113 of 150 answers, while hubspot.com appeared as a cited host in 25. The host total is not a count of links, buyers or completed visits.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Inspect the exact destination before borrowing a statement from an answer. A CRM feature description might point to a product page, a comparison or a video. Keep the page identity and the passage that supports the particular feature. The host label alone cannot establish an integration or a plan entitlement.

Be careful when names resemble one another. Preserve pipedrive.com and pipelinecrm.com as separate records when tracing a source; merging them would erase the distinction retained by the collection. Use the product identity in the answer to decide which document you need to read, then verify that identity at the destination.

For a reporting or forecasting claim, note the edition, date and condition stated in the source. If an answer describes a broader capability than the passage supports, narrow the claim before repeating it. If the source is unavailable, retain an unknown state and choose another readable primary document for any new factual wording.

A CRM publisher can use this list to locate evidence worth inspecting, but the study did not test outreach, paid placement or edits to those sites. Keep your work tied to the specific incorrect statement you can demonstrate. Report the verified page correction separately from any later naming count or referral record.
