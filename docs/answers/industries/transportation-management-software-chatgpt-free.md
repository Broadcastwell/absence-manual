# Is transportation management software with ChatGPT free?

Alvys documents a restricted ChatGPT connection, but its public description does not establish free access to a transportation management system. Our [Agent Gap study](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23197560) reached 4 of 5 transportation pricing destinations on 6 October 2026. Those public terms are a different record from the Free chat functions used to research software.

Observed answers on stated dates, not a ranking.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [Agent Gap study design](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23197560) gave five transportation vendors an initial public-pricing task. Alvys, Oracle Transportation Management, Tai TMS and SAP Transportation Management reached the target; AscendTMS did not. Reaching was recorded separately from correctness. The study did not acquire an account or operate the Alvys connection. In the retained [AscendTMS and Alvys comparison](https://index.broadcastwell.com/questions/transportation-management-systems/ascendtms-vs-alvys-which-is-better-for-a-small-broker/), ChatGPT described Alvys as “Pricing based on monthly load volume” on 22 September 2026. The wording is historical answer text, not a current Alvys price check or evidence that its ChatGPT connection is free.

**Reported.** OpenAI's [Free-plan frequently asked questions (FAQ)](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9275245-chatgpt-free-tier-faq), saved on 30 October 2026, describes public web research subject to feature limits. Alvys's [Intelligence page](https://alvys.com/features/alvys-intelligence), read on 29 October 2026, describes a ChatGPT connection and lists load search, invoice status, driver availability and tracking history.

**Reasoned.** A reached public price page and a permitted query against transport records are separate events. The former does not grant a transport-system account or establish that its documented connection is included in a Free chat plan.

## What this means for you

Read the access conditions. The Alvys section describes bringing an assistant to the company's existing transport records. A research question about freight software starts with public information instead. The reported load-search example therefore concerns a particular account's loads, rather than a free list of loads collected during ordinary web research.

Invoice status has a narrow meaning in the same section. Looking up that status is not described as editing an invoice, approving a charge or sending a customer a bill. Driver availability and tracking history are also listed as queries. The section does not supply a role map or a write-action permission list for those documented operations.

For public research, the [small-carrier question](https://index.broadcastwell.com/questions/transportation-management-systems/what-is-the-best-tms-for-a-small-carrier-with-about-10-trucks/) retains a concrete fleet size of about ten trucks. A mid-size freight brokerage and a large enterprise shipper have separate questions in the panel. These are buyer scenarios in public answers; the Index naming table contains no carrier's private load history or driver-availability record.
