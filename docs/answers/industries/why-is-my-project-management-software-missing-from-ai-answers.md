# Why is my project management software missing from AI answers?

There was no completely absent checked vendor in our project-management category: all ten appeared at least once across 150 valid answers captured on 22 and 23 September 2026. Yet seven of those names were absent from all fifteen answers about combining agile and structured planning. A missing name can therefore be specific to the question. This result does not explain an individual omission or establish a weakness in your product.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [September project-management release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) contains ten questions, three scheduled answers and five engines: ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. All 150 observations were valid. Publication was on 23 September 2026. Every checked vendor appears below, alphabetically, so the positive category totals remain visible.

The combined-planning question had no matches for Airtable, Basecamp, ClickUp, monday.com, Notion, Smartsheet or Wrike across its fifteen valid answers. ## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Identify the work in your question. Coordinating concurrent client projects, keeping a distributed team informed and discussing a planning approach are distinct contexts. Begin with the matching question record before attaching a broad absence label to your software.

Check the roster next. This category can establish that each checked name appeared somewhere, but it provides no result for an unchecked company. A report should show that difference explicitly, alongside the exact question and answer date.

For a missing-name investigation, inspect the answer's factual statements separately from its product list. If a statement about a supported planning function is wrong, compare it with dated product evidence. The seven observed question-level gaps do not prove a content defect or show that changing a page will change a later answer.
