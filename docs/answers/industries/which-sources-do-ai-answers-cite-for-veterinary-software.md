# Which sources do AI answers cite for veterinary software?

A veterinary-software citation can point to a directory, a supplier or another publisher. Answers captured on 22 September 2026 cited capterra.com in 61 of 149 and digitail.com in 52, pooled across five engines. The recorded link is a starting point for checking the adjacent claim, rather than proof that the whole source or product was endorsed.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [September release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) repeated ten veterinary-software questions three times on ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. One scheduled Overview response returned no valid search result, leaving 149 valid answers. Each exact host counts once in an answer. capterra.com's 61 of 149 is 40.9 percent, with a descriptive 95 percent Wilson interval of 33.4 to 49.0 percent. Capture was 22 September 2026; publication followed on 23 September.

Host boundaries matter here: shepherd.vet appeared in 41 answers and help.shepherd.vet in four. software.idexx.com appeared in 40, while idexx.com appeared in seven. Keep those records distinct. The numbers count answer appearances, not individual links, readers or software purchases.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Begin with the practice decision and one factual claim to verify. A source attached to the small-practice comparison may not resolve the question facing a multi-location hospital. Open the saved answer for the relevant situation and preserve the exact destination, rather than searching a domain and assuming the first page was cited.

Identify what kind of page you reached. A directory entry, product description and support article may establish different details about the same system. Note the product identity, stated conditions and date beside the passage. A familiar host name cannot replace that check, and a source without the needed detail leaves the claim unresolved.

Use the subdomain distinction when sharing evidence with colleagues. A support article can be a different document from a supplier's main website. Keep the full destination so another reader can assess the same passage. Do not merge separate hosts merely to create a larger citation figure or attribute all of their pages to one recorded event.

If your team publishes a comparison, correct only what the evidence supports. Record the original claim, the resolving source and the wording you intend to serve. The count does not establish that a page needs changing, that a paid placement would help or that a later answer will select a different vendor. Those are separate questions requiring their own observations.
