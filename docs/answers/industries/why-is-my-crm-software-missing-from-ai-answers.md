# Why is my CRM software missing from AI answers?

Nimble was the only one of ten checked CRM vendors never named in the 150 CRM answers captured on 22 September 2026. That establishes an omission in this collection; the record does not identify why it happened. A zero for one question has a narrower meaning, so first check which observation you are trying to explain.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [September collection](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) asked ten CRM questions three times on each of ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. All 150 answers were valid, and the release was published on 23 September 2026. Nimble's 0 of 150 has a descriptive 95 percent Wilson interval of 0.0 to 2.5 percent. The table keeps all ten checked names visible.

| Checked CRM vendor | Named in valid answers |
| --- | --- |
| Close | 12 of 150 |
| Copper | 4 of 150 |
| Freshsales | 22 of 150 |
| HubSpot CRM | 113 of 150 |
| Insightly | 10 of 150 |
| Microsoft Dynamics 365 | 36 of 150 |
| Nimble | 0 of 150 |
| Pipedrive | 97 of 150 |
| Salesforce Sales Cloud | 109 of 150 |
| Zoho CRM | 73 of 150 |

The question asking what a buyer should evaluate in a CRM left nine of the ten checked vendors unnamed across its fifteen answers. Its question-level base is included in the 150. A criteria question can therefore leave out a brand that appears elsewhere in the collection. Separately, the pooled source counts included pipedrive.com in 43 of 150 answers and hubspot.com in 25; those are host citations, not additional vendor-name events.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Start with the exact CRM decision. A revenue team replacing spreadsheets, a manager choosing forecasting tools and a buyer collecting evaluation criteria should not be treated as one question. Find the corresponding saved answer before deciding that a company-wide visibility problem has been demonstrated.

Check whether the product was in the study's vendor roster and which label was matched. A company outside that roster has no zero row in this table. For a checked vendor, retain its engine, question and answer date; adding another name or alias to a report without checking the matching rule can change the event you claim to measure.

Use cited pages to investigate a factual issue rather than to invent a cause. If a comparison misidentifies your CRM or attributes a feature to the wrong edition, retain the sentence and the primary document that resolves it. If you find no such conflict, say that the reason for the omission remains unknown.

For any supported page edit, record the before-and-after wording and verify the served result. That completes a content change. A later AI answer is a separate observation, and a later visit or enquiry requires another record again.
