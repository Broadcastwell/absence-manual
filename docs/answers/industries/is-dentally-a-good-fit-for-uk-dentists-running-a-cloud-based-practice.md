# Is Dentally a good fit for UK dentists running a cloud based practice?

Dentally is a candidate to examine for a UK cloud-based practice: its UK site describes cloud practice software and NHS or HSC integration coverage, subject to your actual practice requirements. In our dental capture on 22 September 2026, Dentally was named in 0 of 149 valid answers. That observed absence does not establish whether the product suits your practice or whether its advertised connections work.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Separate the two questions. The Index's dental panel asks about small offices, alternatives, product comparisons and purchase criteria. It does not select a UK-only practice sample, so Dentally's zero cannot become a verdict against cloud deployment for your location.

For a UK shortlist, start with the regional claim Dentally actually publishes. NHS in England, Wales and Scotland and HSC in Northern Ireland are named separately. Put your practice's location and intended submission operation beside that claim in the evaluation scope rather than accepting a general UK-compatible label.

Its partner list also distinguishes connected tools from the practice platform. Xero appears as an accounting connection; Dentally Imaging Bridge appears for compatible imaging software. If either matters to your move, inspect the named connection and required setup instead of assuming cloud-based means every existing system is included.

The UK page says integration setup varies by partner and may require enabling it within Dentally or completing setup with that partner. Ask for the applicable steps for the Xero or imaging connection your practice needs, with the current plan and data-migration scope stated in the proposal.
