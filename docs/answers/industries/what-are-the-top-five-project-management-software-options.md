# What are the top five project management software options?

63 of 150 project-management answers named Jira in our [22 and 23 September 2026 Index](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695); the five highest pooled naming totals belong to Asana, ClickUp, Jira, Smartsheet and Wrike. That answers top five by this panel's observed naming frequency. It does not rank their product quality.

Observed answers on stated dates, not a ranking.

## What we measured

**Measured.** We summed the checked-vendor names across five engines and ten questions, with three returns per question and engine in the [published Index release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695). Asana appeared in 97 answers, ClickUp in 80, Jira in 63, Smartsheet in 54 and Wrike in 54. The tied names both belong in the five. The complete alphabetical matrix retains all ten checked products, including their zeros, rather than printing only the most frequent names.

One [ChatGPT answer](https://zenodo.org/records/22907695/files/answers-2026-09.jsonl?download=1) captured on 22 September 2026 includes this wording:

> Jira for a development-led organization, Wrike for resource-intensive delivery, or ClickUp when consolidation and budget are the priorities.

The four non-Overview panels were captured on 22 September. Overviews contributed thirteen returns on 22 September and seventeen on 23 September. Naming totals do not measure product performance or the suitability of a contract for your team.

**Reasoned.** Pooling answers combines several buying situations. The resulting five-name list is an observed shortlist across that collection; it is different from a recommendation made for one specified team.

## What this means for you

Start with the scenario. The [mid-market question](https://index.broadcastwell.com/questions/project-management/what-are-the-top-project-management-tools-for-a-mid-market-organization/) asks for top tools for an organization, while the panel separately asks about cross-functional teams, distributed teams and several concurrent client projects. A supplier can appear under more than one of those situations. The pooled total preserves those appearances without pretending the scenarios are interchangeable.

There is also a difference between a frequent product and a universal answer. Jira appears eleven times in Claude's thirty returns and eighteen in ChatGPT's. Asana appears twenty-one times in ChatGPT and twenty-three in Mode. Those are engine-specific naming results inside the same dated collection, rather than an implementation comparison between Jira and Asana.

Keep both tied products. Smartsheet and Wrike each total fifty-four names, although their engine rows differ. Smartsheet has seven ChatGPT names and ten Overview names; Wrike has six ChatGPT names and nine Overview names.
