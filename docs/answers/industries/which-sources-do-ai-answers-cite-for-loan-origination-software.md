# Which sources do AI answers cite for loan origination software?

The loan-origination source records include loanpro.io in 55 of 150 valid answers captured on 22 September 2026, aloan.ai in 47 and ncino.com in 33. The full category contains 180 distinct cited hosts. These are places to begin checking an answer's lending-software claims, rather than a measured explanation of how an engine selected a product.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [September lending-software panel](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) kept ten questions and three scheduled runs on each of five engines. Its 150 answers were all valid. We recounted the attached source URLs across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode, counting an exact host at most once in each answer. Publication was on 23 September 2026, following the 22 September captures.

loanpro.io's 55 appearances correspond to 36.7 percent of answer source lists. The descriptive 95 percent Wilson interval is 29.4 to 44.6 percent. This measures recorded citation presence under the fixed question design, without measuring lending decisions or a vendor's influence.

These six alphabetical rows are selected examples, not an inventory of all 180 hosts. Several can occur in one answer. Their counts therefore cannot be added to obtain a unique-answer total or converted into a market share. The figures pool engines and should retain that scope in any report.

The source uses exact host labels. For example, investor.ncino.com occurred in two answer source lists, separately from ncino.com's 33. A page about a supplier and a page describing a product function may serve different purposes; the host count does not settle what the cited passage supports.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Find the question that resembles the institution you are investigating. A community bank, credit union and mortgage broker do not give a software comparison the same context. Keep the saved answer with the source URLs before deciding which claim to verify.

Follow the precise page reference. If the answer asserts a product connection, an edition or a supported task, locate the passage that makes that claim and note its date. A source's frequent appearance cannot substitute for evidence about the specific statement.

For a client report, separate the count from the inspection result. You can report that loanpro.io was cited in 55 answers and separately describe whether one cited passage supported one statement. Neither observation establishes a completed transaction, a product's suitability or the reason another lender's software was omitted.
