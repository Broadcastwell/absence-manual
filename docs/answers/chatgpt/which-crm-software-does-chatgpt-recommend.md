# Which CRM software does ChatGPT recommend?

HubSpot CRM appeared in 24 of 30 ChatGPT CRM answers captured on 22 September 2026; Salesforce Sales Cloud appeared in 21 and Pipedrive in 17. That is an observed naming list, rather than a tested recommendation for your sales process. The question-level records help distinguish a team replacing spreadsheets from one managing a complex pipeline.

## What we measured

**Measured.** Ten fixed CRM questions, repeated three times on ChatGPT, supplied the 30 valid answers in this [September release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695). HubSpot's 24 appearances give 80.0 percent, with a descriptive 95 percent Wilson interval of 62.7 to 90.5 percent. Captures are dated 22 September 2026; publication followed on 23 September.

On the spreadsheet-replacement question, HubSpot CRM and Pipedrive appeared in all three answers while Salesforce Sales Cloud appeared in none. On the complex-pipeline question, HubSpot CRM, Salesforce Sales Cloud and Microsoft Dynamics 365 each appeared in all three. These small question-level counts show the recorded context, without explaining why ChatGPT generated a particular list. More than one vendor can appear in an answer, so the table is not market share.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Write down the sales decision before reading the counts. Moving contacts out of a spreadsheet may call for a different evaluation from coordinating several sales motions. Use the matching public question to choose which names deserve further research, then retain the requirements your team actually needs.

Turn each shortlisted name into a source check. For reporting, forecasting or handoff claims, identify the product edition and the documentation that establishes the feature. The count beside HubSpot CRM does not verify an integration, implementation effort or commercial term. Ask for evidence of the exact operation you expect the software to perform.

For a visibility report, keep ChatGPT's 30-answer base intact. Copper, Freshsales, Insightly and Nimble each have zero recorded naming here, which describes this collection rather than their availability. If you compare two CRM vendors, inspect their actual question records; aggregate totals cannot tell you which answers contained both.
