# Which insurance agency software does ChatGPT recommend?

Applied Systems is the checked vendor label in this record, and it appeared in 27 of 30 ChatGPT insurance-agency answers captured on 22 September 2026. EZLynx appeared in 21 and Vertafore in 20. The broad label must stay intact: its count does not establish a recommendation for every Applied product or every type of agency.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [published September collection](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) used ten insurance-agency buyer questions with three ChatGPT answers per question. All 30 were valid. Applied Systems's 27 of 30 gives 90.0 percent, with a descriptive 95 percent Wilson interval of 74.4 to 96.5 percent. The observation date was 22 September 2026; the release followed on 23 September.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Identify the line of business before you use the aggregate. The recorded questions distinguish a small property-and-casualty agency, a growing commercial-lines brokerage and a life-and-health benefits agency. An overall total can conceal that decision, so open the relevant question instead of treating all agency-management work as interchangeable.

Translate a broad vendor label into a precise product investigation. Name the product, module and task you want to verify, then read the source that supports it. A parent-company appearance cannot establish a carrier connection, a workflow or a commercial condition for every offering under that name. Keep any unresolved fact explicit until you have the right documentation.

For a supplier comparison, retain the original checked labels and the alphabetical order. The table does not rank vendors by operational suitability, customer numbers or endorsement. If your own agency software has a zero row, use that dated observation to inspect the question coverage before proposing an explanation for the omission.
