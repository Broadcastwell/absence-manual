# Which project management software does ChatGPT recommend?

All ten checked project-management vendors were named at least once in the thirty ChatGPT answers captured on 22 September 2026. Asana appeared in 21 of 30 and Jira in 18. Those appearances show the names returned for the study's questions, without ranking which platform would fit your team's work.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [September release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) retained thirty valid ChatGPT answers from three runs of ten fixed project-management questions. Asana's 21 of 30 is 70.0%, with a descriptive 95% Wilson interval of 52.1% to 83.3%. The release was published on 23 September 2026; the ChatGPT observations in this category were captured on 22 September.

## What this means for you

Decide which coordination problem you need to investigate. The fixed bank includes a cross-functional team, a distributed team and a services business handling concurrent client projects. Their category total is useful context, but the question-level record is closer to each decision.

Avoid turning table order into preference. Alphabetical rows keep Asana, Jira and the other checked names visible without manufacturing an endorsement score. Check a candidate's relevant workflow, edition and current terms from product sources before comparing it with your requirements.

If your report concerns a current brand, preserve the exact question and engine as you compare dates. A name appearing in more answers after a question-set change is a different observation, rather than proof that the product became more suitable.
