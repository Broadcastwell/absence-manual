# Which dental software does ChatGPT recommend?

Dentrix and Open Dental tied at 22 of 30 valid ChatGPT dental answers captured on 22 September 2026; Dentrix Ascend appeared in 21. This engine's base is 30, despite an exclusion elsewhere in the multi-engine dental collection. The record counts names and preserves product labels; it does not establish a clinical or software-quality recommendation.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [September study](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) asked ten dental buyer questions three times on ChatGPT and retained every answer in that engine slice. Dentrix's 22 of 30 gives 73.3 percent, with a descriptive 95 percent Wilson interval of 55.6 to 85.8 percent. Collection took place on 22 September 2026, before the 23 September publication.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Match the practice decision to the question before using the tied total. Starting a practice, running a very small office and choosing a system for several locations are different tasks in this panel. The 22-to-22 tie does not choose between products for any one of those tasks.

Create a comparison with the exact product label on each row. For a required connection or feature, attach the relevant vendor documentation and any plan or deployment condition. A description of Dentrix should not silently become a claim about Dentrix Ascend. The same care applies when an answer refers loosely to a parent company or product family.

Keep reporting bases specific to the engine. This ChatGPT table uses 30 valid answers throughout; importing the dental category's 149-answer multi-engine base or AI Overviews' 29-answer base would change the meaning. Zero rows stay visible as recorded absences, without treating them as a judgment on patient care or a practice's operational requirements.
