# Which field service software does ChatGPT recommend?

Housecall Pro appeared in 21 of 30 ChatGPT field service answers captured on 22 September 2026, but in none of three answers about commercial mechanical and fire-protection contractors. ServiceTitan appeared in 19 of 30 overall and all three of those commercial answers. The distinction gives you a context for researching names, without proving that either product fits the work.

## What we measured

**Measured.** Under the [September Index method](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695), ten field service questions were each observed three times on ChatGPT. All 30 observations were valid. Housecall Pro's 21 appearances are 70.0 percent, with a descriptive 95 percent Wilson interval of 52.1 to 83.3 percent. Captures are from 22 September 2026; the release date is 23 September.

These are parts of the same dated panel, and the matching rule records whether a vendor name appears. It does not score dispatch performance, implementation success or an endorsement's strength.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Put the trade and size of the operation into the question you inspect. A one-person HVAC business and a contractor serving commercial sites can have different needs even when both fall under field service management. Find the recorded question that is relevant before carrying a name into procurement.

Build a short task checklist for that operation. If scheduling, service agreements or a particular connection matters, seek a source that supports it for the exact product. Use a demonstration or product documentation to assess the requirement; the observed name count cannot confirm that the work is supported under your conditions.

For a vendor page, state the supported trade and product boundary rather than treating the broad category as a claim about every contractor. When explaining a missing name, preserve the dated question and captured answer. The panel provides a recorded gap to inspect, not evidence of why the engine omitted the supplier or what website change would alter it.
