# Which radiology software does ChatGPT recommend?

For the radiology questions in our 22 September 2026 collection, ChatGPT named Intelerad in 15 of 30 valid answers and RamSoft in 12. Eighteen of twenty-four checked vendors appeared at least once. This is a dated software-name observation, without a clinical-performance or product-suitability recommendation.

## What we measured

## What this means for you

Separate the software question from a clinical claim. The fixed questions include an outpatient imaging centre, a five-location group, a hospital system and a teleradiology firm. A name returned for one setting does not establish its suitability for another.

Keep product identity precise as you investigate. A PACS, a RIS and a broader imaging platform describe different scopes; the naming count cannot verify the capabilities or deployment terms you need. Use appropriate dated primary product evidence for those details.

Treat the zero rows as observations about this checked sample. They do not show that a vendor lacks a particular function, fails a clinical standard or is unavailable. The study did not test those outcomes, and it did not measure what any clinical team purchased.
