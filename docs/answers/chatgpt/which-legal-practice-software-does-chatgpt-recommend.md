# Which legal practice software does ChatGPT recommend?

For a personal-injury firm, the relevant ChatGPT question named Clio in three of three answers and 8am MyCase in none. Across the broader [legal-practice panel](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695), Clio appeared in 25 of 30 answers and 8am MyCase in 18. Both results come from 22 September 2026; the narrower question should remain visible beside the totals, which do not assess either product's fitness for legal work.

Observed answers on stated dates, not a ranking.

## What we measured

**Measured.** Three ChatGPT captures of each of ten legal-practice questions produced 30 valid answers in the [September Index](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695). Clio's 25 of 30 is 83.3 percent, with a descriptive 95 percent Wilson interval of 66.4 to 92.7 percent, a statistical range around the observed share. In one retained [answer record](https://zenodo.org/records/22907695/files/answers-2026-09.jsonl?download=1), ChatGPT responded to “what is the best case management software for a personal injury firm” on 22 September 2026, run 1, with the fragment “Clio with personal injury features”. This historical wording is an answer observation, not independent verification of the product claim or an explanation of the aggregate count.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Use the type of practice and the actual work to choose a question. The panel spans a one-attorney office, a small firm, personal-injury case management and a litigation firm moving away from on-premises software. Those contexts should remain attached to the names that arose from them.

Set the product check at the level of the requirement. If the decision concerns a connection, record handling or another operational task, find documentation for the exact product and applicable terms. Do not let a fluent AI description stand in for that evidence. This collection did not assess professional obligations, the handling of client material or suitability for an individual firm.

For a comparison page or internal report, distinguish the dated name count from your conclusion about a product. A zero row is a result within these questions, not proof that a vendor is unavailable. A high count does not remove the need for your own documented evaluation, and the study cannot identify which website edit would cause a future answer to change.
