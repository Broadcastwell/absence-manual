# Which legal practice software does ChatGPT recommend?

Clio appeared in 25 of 30 ChatGPT legal-practice answers captured on 22 September 2026, and 8am MyCase in 18. On the personal-injury-firm question, Clio appeared in all three answers while 8am MyCase appeared in none. That is a practice-specific naming observation, without an assessment of either product's fitness for legal work.

## What we measured

**Measured.** Three ChatGPT captures of each of ten legal-practice questions produced 30 valid answers in the [September Index](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695). ## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Use the type of practice and the actual work to choose a question. The panel spans a one-attorney office, a small firm, personal-injury case management and a litigation firm moving away from on-premise software. Those contexts should remain attached to the names that arose from them.

Set the product check at the level of the requirement. If the decision concerns a connection, record handling or another operational task, find documentation for the exact product and applicable terms. Do not let a fluent AI description stand in for that evidence. This collection did not assess professional obligations, the handling of client material or suitability for an individual firm.

For a comparison page or internal report, distinguish the dated name count from your conclusion about a product. A zero row is a result within these questions, not proof that a vendor is unavailable. A high count does not remove the need for your own documented evaluation, and the study cannot identify which website edit would cause a future answer to change.
