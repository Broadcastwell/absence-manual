# Which loan origination software does ChatGPT recommend?

The captured ChatGPT answers named MeridianLink in 17 of 30 valid loan-origination answers and nCino in 15, on 22 September 2026. These are observed responses to the fixed buyer questions, including recommendation-style questions. The naming rule does not establish that either product is the right recommendation for a particular lender.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [September method](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) used ten lending-software questions with three scheduled ChatGPT runs each. All thirty answers in this engine slice were valid. MeridianLink's 17 of 30 corresponds to 56.7%, with a descriptive 95% Wilson interval of 39.2% to 72.6%. Sixteen of the twenty-five checked vendors were named at least once.

## What this means for you

The buyer's institution matters. This question bank includes a community bank, a credit union, a mortgage broker and a lender funding specialty loans in house. Start with the actual question that resembles your lending operation before carrying a name into a procurement discussion.

Keep commercial and operational requirements separate from the count. A product name in an answer does not verify a lending workflow, deployment condition, integration or current price. Those claims need evidence at the product and institution level.

Read the source links in the answer when available. This engine-specific table records naming only; a publisher mentioned in a pooled source list is not automatically a source cited by ChatGPT for your question. Nor can a source's presence establish the reason a product was named.
