# Which construction software does ChatGPT recommend?

A small-builder question can produce a different starting list from a category total. In ChatGPT answers captured on 22 September 2026, Procore appeared in 23 of 30 construction answers and Buildertrend in 17. Those naming observations help identify products to investigate; the study did not grade ChatGPT's endorsement or the products' suitability.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [September Index protocol](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) collected three ChatGPT answers for each of ten construction buyer questions. All 30 were valid, and the release was published on 23 September 2026. Procore's 23 of 30 is 76.7 percent, with a descriptive 95 percent Wilson interval of 59.1 to 88.2 percent.

That question is part of the 30-answer total, not an independent comparison group. The alphabetical table counts each checked vendor once per answer, including names with no recorded appearance.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Start with your role in the project. A builder choosing estimating and scheduling software has a different decision from an owner overseeing capital projects. The published panel includes both kinds of question. Locate the one that resembles your work before taking a name from the larger total.

For each product you retain, write the required task beside a current product source: estimating, document exchange, subcontractor coordination or another defined job. A repeated name does not settle whether that function is native, available in your edition or suitable for your project. An AI answer can introduce the name; the supporting product evidence must carry the capability claim.

If you publish a construction software comparison, preserve the question and capture date alongside this count. Explain an observed omission only when you have evidence for its cause. A zero in these answers does not establish that a supplier lacks the relevant function, and a new page edit does not by itself prove a later naming change.
