# Which property management software does ChatGPT recommend?

ChatGPT named Buildium in 24 of 30 property-management answers and AppFolio in 18, captured on 22 September 2026. Thirteen of the twenty-two checked vendors appeared at least once. These names reflect the study's residential-property questions, with no finding that the more frequently named product suits every portfolio.

## What we measured

## What this means for you

Locate the portfolio condition before using a category count. The bank includes a small landlord, a landlord with fifty units, a portfolio of about one hundred units and a growing management company. Those are different research tasks even when the same vendor appears in several answers.

Check the required work with appropriate product evidence. A repeated name does not establish a rent-accounting arrangement, maintenance function, plan condition or price. Keep the specific edition and scope attached to any factual comparison.

For a vendor absent from this panel, investigate the question-level answers before attributing a cause. The study contains a defined checked set and dated observations. It cannot establish that a website edit would add a name, or that the absence explains a landlord's actual choice.
