# Which dealership software does ChatGPT recommend?

For an independent used-car dealer, the captured ChatGPT answers named DealerCenter, Frazer and AutoManager in all three runs of that question. Across the full dealership panel on 22 September 2026, DealerCenter appeared in 16 of 30 answers and CDK Global in 15. These are naming counts, not a verdict that either system meets your dealership's requirements.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The dealership panel in the [published September method](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) contains ten questions and three ChatGPT runs per question. It retained all 30 planned answers. DealerCenter's 16 of 30 is 53.3 percent, with a descriptive 95 percent Wilson interval of 36.1 to 69.8 percent. The answers were captured on 22 September 2026 and released on 23 September.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Choose the dealership type first. An independent lot, a franchise store and a dealer group running several rooftops need different questions. Carry that context into your research list rather than selecting a system solely because its name accumulated more appearances across the mixed panel.

Read the exact product name when following an answer to a source. A broad vendor statement may not establish what a particular DMS module includes. Ask the supplier to substantiate the operations your store needs, such as inventory handling, accounting or a required connection. None of those capabilities was tested by the name-matching measure here.

For a dealership software page, make the supported store type and product scope easy to verify. Keep an unverified feature in a research note until its source is clear. If you report an omitted vendor, retain the question and zero count as the observation; the absence alone does not diagnose product fit or the reason ChatGPT left it out.
