# How do Google AI Overviews choose sources?

Our [Answer RFP capture](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23271935) recorded capterra.com in the citation URLs of 45 of 414 included Google AI Overviews from 22 and 23 September 2026. Google says supporting pages must be indexed and eligible for a Search snippet; our citation observations show which sources appeared, without establishing why Google chose an individual page.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [published Answer RFP method and records](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23271935) contain 414 included Overview answers. We count a Capterra host at most once within each answer, even if its URL list contains several Capterra pages. Ten included Overview records have empty citation-URL lists. Team review of this derived study remains in progress.

**Reported.** Google’s [AI features guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), read on 29 October 2026, says a supporting page must be indexed and eligible to appear with a snippet in Search. It describes related searches across subtopics and data sources, with supporting links identified while responses are generated. Meeting requirements does not guarantee crawling, indexing or serving.

**Reasoned.** Capterra’s recorded appearances are evidence about returned sources. They do not reveal Google’s internal selection weights or prove that a page format caused a citation.

## What this means for you

Read the actual link. In the second Overview run of the construction question about Procore alternatives, the recorded Capterra URL ends in /p/56250/Procore/alternatives/. That answer’s list also includes Kahua and Drawboard alternative pages. It is a particular comparison-source set, rather than evidence that the Capterra domain was the only source selected.

An empty recorded list needs different treatment. Those ten Overview answers remain in the 414-answer included cohort, but their stored URL fields cannot tell you which sources a reader might have seen elsewhere in the experience. Marking them as evidence that Google consulted no source would claim more than the record preserves.

The domain total also spans different categories. The included veterinary Overview records contain fourteen Capterra host observations from twenty-nine answers; construction contributes one from thirty included answers. These are category cuts of this derived study, with different included bases, rather than equal-sized shares of the forty-five observations.

For your CRM source review, the first Overview run of buyer-evaluation question nine records Capterra’s /resources/crm-software-for-real-estate/ page. Its path identifies the particular resource behind that host observation. The other recorded links include buying guides from CRM Switch and Tomorrow’s Office.
