# Google AI answers

AI Overviews, AI Mode, their different answers and the bases needed to compare them.

These 12 answers use dated Broadcastwell findings. Each page identifies its question, figure and source; a count in an answer does not establish product quality or a completed sale.

## Google surfaces

[What is a Google AI Overview?](what-is-a-google-ai-overview.md)

An AI Overview is a Google Search summary with supporting links. In a 4 August 2026 study, 258 of 280 main searches returned one and 22 did not.

[What is Google AI Mode?](what-is-google-ai-mode.md)

AI Mode is Google’s AI Search experience for deeper questions. It named Procore in 3 of 3 answers to one construction question on 22 September 2026.

[How do Google AI Overviews and AI Mode compare?](how-do-google-ai-overviews-and-ai-mode-compare.md)

The Google experiences can return different names: on 22 September 2026, Procore appeared in 0 of 3 Overview answers and 3 of 3 AI Mode answers.

## Search controls

[Does Google-Extended control AI Overview or AI Mode visibility?](does-google-extended-control-ai-overview-or-ai-mode-visibility.md)

Google-Extended is separate from Search controls. Our 4 August 2026 collection recorded 258 Overviews in 280 searches, without testing bot policies.

## Source selection

[How does Google AI Mode choose cited sources?](how-does-google-ai-mode-choose-cited-sources.md)

Google reports related-search and eligibility mechanisms, not a complete selection formula. Procore’s 3 of 3 AI Mode mentions measure naming only.

## Counting answers

[Should AI Overview rates include searches without an Overview?](should-ai-overview-rates-include-searches-without-an-overview.md)

Include no-Overview searches in a trigger rate; use displayed answers for a conditional naming rate. The August collection had 258 Overviews in 280 searches.
