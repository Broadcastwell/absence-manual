# Which veterinary software does Google AI Mode name?

ezyVet appeared in 24 of 30 Google AI Mode answers in the veterinary software panel captured on 22 September 2026. The combined checked label IDEXX (Cornerstone, Neo) appeared in 22, while DaySmart Vet appeared in 15. Those counts describe naming, without independently testing a clinic's required capabilities.

## What we measured

**Measured.** Three runs of ten veterinary buyer questions produced 30 valid AI Mode answers in the [September release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695). ezyVet's 24 of 30 is 80.0 percent, with a Wilson 95 percent interval of 62.7 to 90.5 percent. ## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Find the question that resembles your clinic's decision. A new-clinic question can be useful for starting research, but the count does not replace a list of the clinic's actual requirements. Keep the observed question and the intended use together as you inspect the named products.

Check a specific product claim at the same level as the source. Where a record groups names, do not split the group's observation into separate product results. Where a capability concerns one edition or connection, use evidence for that edition or connection instead of borrowing a broader brand statement.

- **If you assess veterinary software:** verify the exact product and required function after using the observed names to guide research.
- **If you maintain a product page:** make any product boundary or dependency explicit and link the supporting documentation.
- **If you report these counts:** preserve the grouped IDEXX label and the 30-answer AI Mode denominator.

This study did not assess clinical performance, suitability for an individual practice or the effect of changing a vendor page. A missing name remains a dated observation to investigate, rather than a diagnosis of why a clinic might choose differently.
