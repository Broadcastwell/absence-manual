# Which dental practice software does Google AI Overviews name?

Dentrix and Open Dental were each named in 24 of 29 valid Google AI Overviews answers about dental practice software captured on 22 September 2026. Curve Dental appeared in 23. The 29-answer base matters: one of the 30 planned AI Overviews observations was excluded.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [published September method](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) scheduled three runs of ten dental buyer questions. After the recorded exclusion, AI Overviews contributed 29 valid answers. Dentrix's 24 of 29 is 82.8 percent, with a Wilson 95 percent interval of 65.5 to 92.4 percent. These captures are dated 22 September 2026 and were released 23 September.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Begin with the type of practice and the decision described by the buyer question. Then inspect which names appeared for that question. A combined dental total can hide a difference between a startup-practice question and a different practice-management task.

Keep product identity precise when you move from this table to a feature comparison. Dentrix, Dentrix Ascend and Dentrix Enterprise have separate checked labels here. A naming count attached to one label does not establish the capabilities, plan or deployment conditions of another.

- **If you run a practice:** use relevant observed names as the start of a factual comparison, then check the current requirements of your practice.
- **If you write a dental software page:** put the exact product name and supported scope beside the statement a buyer will read.
- **If you publish the figures:** keep the excluded observation visible and print 24 of 29 rather than silently using 30 as the denominator.

The zero-count rows remain part of the checked set. Their absence from these answers is not a clinical, operational or software-quality assessment, and the study does not make those decisions for a practice.
