# Which CRM software does Google AI Overviews name?

HubSpot CRM and Salesforce Sales Cloud each appeared in 20 of 30 Google AI Overviews CRM answers captured on 22 September 2026. Pipedrive appeared in 18. Equal totals do not show that the products appeared together or that Google judged them equally suitable.

## What we measured

**Measured.** Ten fixed CRM buyer questions were each asked three times under the [September Index protocol](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695). This AI Overviews slice retained 30 valid answers. HubSpot's 20 of 30 is 66.7 percent, with a Wilson 95 percent interval of 48.8 to 80.8 percent. The observations are from 22 September 2026; the release was published 23 September.

The table is alphabetical so that a measured count does not become a recommendation order. A naming event is counted once for a vendor in a valid answer. Several vendors can share an answer, and the totals alone cannot reveal exactly where two vendors overlapped.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Use the buyer question to decide which CRM names deserve a closer read. A growing sales team, a simpler contact-management need and a specific evaluation task can represent different decisions. The full question panel preserves those distinctions more clearly than a single total.

When two totals tie, keep the tie. Do not invent a preference from the table's row order or turn the counts into a product-quality verdict. For an overlap question, inspect the question-level records and retain the underlying observations where available; the category totals are insufficient on their own.

- **If you select a CRM:** compare your requirements and dated product evidence after identifying relevant names.
- **If you prepare a comparison:** distinguish the checked vendor identity from a plan, edition or integration claim that needs separate support.
- **If you track an existing CRM brand:** keep this AI Overviews view apart from observations collected on AI Mode or another engine.

Copper and Nimble each have zero recorded naming here. Those zeros are findings about this 30-answer collection, not a complete account of their availability, customers or suitability.
