# What are the top 3 CRM platforms?

In our [22 September 2026 CRM Index](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695), the highest naming totals belong to HubSpot CRM, Pipedrive and Salesforce Sales Cloud. Salesforce appeared in 109 of 150 valid answers. Here, top means most often named in this dated buyer-question panel, rather than the best product for every business.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [original September release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) covers ten CRM buyer questions, three returns and five engines. We counted a checked vendor at most once per answer. The three highest totals below are listed alphabetically.

| Checked CRM | Answers naming it | Valid-answer base |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HubSpot CRM | 113 | 150 |
| Pipedrive | 97 | 150 |
| Salesforce Sales Cloud | 109 | 150 |

Multiple vendors can appear in one answer, so these are not exclusive shares of a market or a pool of customers.

**Reasoned.** The same panel can identify frequently selected names and preserve the different contexts producing them. It does not test product quality, current pricing or which CRM fits your business.

## What this means for you

Keep the question visible. The panel asks about a growing B2B sales team, replacing spreadsheets, vendor alternatives, forecasting and sales-to-customer-success handoffs. A supplier can collect appearances from several of those contexts. Its pooled total is therefore broader than the answer to any one of them.

The [small-sales-team question](https://index.broadcastwell.com/questions/crm/which-crm-options-work-well-for-a-small-sales-team-that-is-scaling/) lets you inspect a narrower observation. Its Perplexity row names HubSpot CRM, Pipedrive, Salesforce Sales Cloud and Zoho CRM in each of the three returns, while Freshsales appears once. That row preserves several alternatives instead of selecting just the highest pooled name.

The lower pooled counts remain part of the same release: Zoho CRM has seventy-three naming answers, Microsoft Dynamics 365 thirty-six and Freshsales twenty-two.
