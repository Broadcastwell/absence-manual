# What skills help with ChatGPT SEO work?

If you use ChatGPT for SEO (search engine optimization) product research, practice reading qualifiers, tracing references and distinguishing unknowns from errors. Our [Wrong About You study](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23270124) flags 4 of 30 JobTread statements as wrong, with answer captures from 22 September 2026 and references read on 8 October.

Observed answers on stated dates, not a ranking.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [published statement-review method](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23270124) classifies JobTread's thirty statements as twenty-five true, four wrong and one unverifiable, with none stale. Three wrong entries concern pricing; the fourth says a free trial is offered. The retained pricing-page reference includes billing and internal-user terms and a separate explanation about trials. Team review: in progress. The study did not test a training course or a person's skill level.

The retained historical Claude [statement](https://index.broadcastwell.com/vendors/jobtread/construction-project-management/#wrong-about-you), captured on 22 September 2026 and marked wrong against the dated vendor reference, reads:

> Pricing starts at $199/month billed annually for one internal user.

This is the disputed historical statement, not a current vendor price. Team review: in progress.

**Reported.** Google's [AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), read on 29 October 2026, identifies indexing and snippet permission for supporting links. Those published-page conditions are separate from checking a generated product fact.

**Reasoned.** The JobTread entries supply concrete reading and reporting exercises, while the Google conditions identify another technical task that a generated draft cannot establish by its wording alone.

## What this means for you

Read the billing phrase. One JobTread answer says its starting amount is billed annually; the retained pricing reference separately describes a monthly charge, internal users and an annual-subscription saving. The source-reading task is to preserve which qualifier belongs to which amount, rather than copying an isolated figure into a comparison.

Follow the negative statement. Another retained answer says the compared products offer free trials. The JobTread pricing-page extract instead explicitly says it does not offer one and gives a customer-support reason. A reader needs to distinguish that particular vendor's reference from a sentence making a claim about several products together.
