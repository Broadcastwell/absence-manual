# What does Searchable offer for AI search visibility?

Searchable describes an AI-search platform with answer monitoring, a brand-focused agent, content creation and site audits. To interpret its measures, keep the question set visible: our [22 September 2026 CRM release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) (customer relationship management) named Zoho CRM in 15 of 30 ChatGPT answers. That own observation illustrates a bounded naming measure; it is not a result produced by Searchable.

Observed answers on stated dates, not a ranking.

## What we measured

**Measured.** Our [CRM release CSV](https://index.broadcastwell.com/data/crm-2026-09.csv) separates five engine-specific naming counts. Zoho CRM’s ChatGPT count is fifteen from thirty valid returns. The same product’s all-engine result is 73 of 150 answers. Its 23/150 citation field counts answers that both name the checked product and cite its owned domain; it does not count every raw Zoho URL in the entire source list.

One [ChatGPT answer](https://zenodo.org/records/22907695/files/answers-2026-09.jsonl?download=1) captured on 22 September 2026 includes this wording:

> HubSpot, Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Zoho CRM, and Pipedrive

**Reported.** Searchable’s [homepage](https://www.searchable.com/), read on 30 October 2026, describes its AEO Insights feature for answer engine optimization (AEO). Its fields cover visibility, mentions and citations, sentiment, and sources. It also presents a brand-specific agent, Content Studio, and technical optimization. These are published descriptions of its offer. Our release did not test Searchable’s account, generated content, integrations or performance.

**Reasoned.** Match a displayed platform measure to its stated unit and prompt population. A product feature description and a measured brand outcome are different evidence.

## What this means for you

Searchable places Content Studio beside its monitoring tools. Its public example is an electric-mountain-bike comparison outline with heading structure and a model table. That illustration shows the kind of content planning the supplier promotes; it does not establish that publishing the example increased citations. Read the actual output and its sources before using a generated product claim.

Before reading a Searchable view as a brand result, identify the questions and engines it includes. In our separate Zoho example dated 22 September 2026, replacing fifteen names in thirty ChatGPT answers with the combined 73 of 150 all-engine answers would change the population rather than show an improvement.

For the Sources view, distinguish a supplier’s own host from a product name in an answer. The CRM release lists help.zoho.com and zoho.com among the product’s cited domains, while the concurrent product-name citation base remains 150.
