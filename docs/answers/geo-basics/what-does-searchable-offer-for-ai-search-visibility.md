# What does Searchable offer for AI search visibility?

Searchable describes an AI-search platform with answer monitoring, a brand-focused agent, content creation and site audits. To interpret its measures, keep the question set visible: our [22 September 2026 CRM release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) named Zoho CRM in 15 of 30 ChatGPT answers. That own observation illustrates a bounded naming measure; it is not a result produced by Searchable.

## What we measured

**Measured.** Our [CRM release CSV](https://index.broadcastwell.com/data/crm-2026-09.csv) separates five engine-specific naming counts. Zoho CRM’s ChatGPT count is fifteen from thirty valid returns. The same product’s all-engine result is 73 of 150 answers. Its 23/150 citation field counts answers that both name the checked product and cite its owned domain; it does not count every raw Zoho URL in the entire source list.

**Reported.** Searchable’s [homepage](https://www.searchable.com/), read on 30 October 2026, describes AEO Insights fields for visibility, mentions and citations, sentiment, and sources. It also presents a brand-specific agent, Content Studio, and technical optimisation. These are published descriptions of its offer. Our release did not test Searchable’s account, generated content, integrations or performance.

**Reasoned.** Match a displayed platform measure to its stated unit and prompt population. A product feature description and a measured brand outcome are different evidence.

## What this means for you

Searchable places Content Studio beside its monitoring tools. Its public example is an electric-mountain-bike comparison outline with heading structure and a model table. That illustration shows the kind of content planning the supplier promotes; it does not establish that publishing the example increased citations. Read the actual output and its sources before using a generated product claim.

The site also presents Prompt Universe as a way to choose questions and an MCP connection for querying AI-search data. Ask which prompts and engines are included in the particular exported view you receive. In our separate Zoho example, replacing fifteen ChatGPT names with the combined 73 would change the population rather than show an improvement.

For the Sources view, distinguish a supplier’s own host from a product name in an answer. The CRM release lists help.zoho.com and zoho.com among the product’s cited domains, while the concurrent product-name citation base remains 150.
