# What can Perplexity Computer do for SEO?

Perplexity describes Computer as a way to research, use a browser and assemble work across tools. An SEO (search engine optimization) use would be a bounded site review with inspectable sources. Public-page tasks can still stop short: our [6 October 2026 Agent Gap study method](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23197560) recorded 0 of 5 initial loan-software pricing tasks reaching their required destination. It was a separate public-site task study, not a test of Perplexity Computer.

Observed answers on stated dates, not a ranking.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [Agent Gap study method](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23197560) keeps finding a destination, interpreting it correctly and reaching the required handoff as separate fields. In the five initial loan-origination pricing-path sessions, none reached the required destination, while all five observations were classified correct. A correct observation and a completed path were therefore different outcomes.

This is a historical public-site task result. It does not benchmark Computer, test an SEO campaign or establish that a current website remains inaccessible.

**Reported.** The [Computer product page](https://www.perplexity.ai/products/computer), read on 30 October 2026, describes web research, browser actions, extraction, reports and recurring tasks. It also describes connections to other tools. Those are provider capability statements, not actions completed in this article.

**Reasoned.** A useful brief might ask Computer to list a site’s public category pages, collect their titles and canonical links, and identify inconsistent product claims. Require a source URL for each finding. Keep publishing, account access and recurring activity outside the task unless separately authorized.

## What this means for you

The initial nCino pricing task gives the aggregate a concrete result. On 6 October 2026, its public pricing destination was not reached, while the observation was classified correct. No vendor login wall or challenge was recorded. The result does not establish that nCino had no pricing information; it describes the destination outcome in that dated task.

Keep the failed destination. A proposed Computer review could begin with that same public pricing-path question and return a report containing the requested URL, the page actually read and any reported retrieval failure. Computer's product page explicitly describes browser actions, data extraction and reports, which fit that deliverable. The historical nCino record supplies the reason the report needs a stopping-point field instead of a populated price at any cost.

Computer also advertises recurring tasks. For this particular review, a proposed recurrence would revisit the named public pricing destination and compare its recorded access state with the earlier report.
