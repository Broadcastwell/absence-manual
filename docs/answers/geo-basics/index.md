# GEO basics answers

Definitions, question sets and the difference between being cited and being chosen.

These 15 answers use dated Broadcastwell findings. Each page identifies its question, figure and source; a count in an answer does not establish product quality or a completed sale.

## Definitions

[What does AI visibility mean?](what-does-ai-visibility-mean.md)

AI visibility needs a defined event: Housecall Pro was named in 108 of 150 answers, while 43 also cited its domain.

[What does GEO mean in AI search?](what-does-geo-mean-in-ai-search.md)

## Reading shortlists

[What is an AI software shortlist?](what-is-an-ai-software-shortlist.md)

[Does an AI brand mention mean the product was recommended?](does-an-ai-brand-mention-mean-the-product-was-recommended.md)

[Does being named by AI prove product quality?](does-being-named-by-ai-prove-product-quality.md)

## Question conditions

[Why does the buyer question matter to an AI shortlist?](why-does-the-buyer-question-matter-to-an-ai-shortlist.md)

[Do brand-name prompts measure discovery?](do-brand-name-prompts-measure-discovery.md)

## Reading sources

[What can a cited domain tell me about an AI source?](what-can-a-cited-domain-tell-me-about-an-ai-source.md)

A domain identifies a host, not an author or proof of a claim. Medium appeared in 6 of 150 project-management answers captured in September 2026.

## Fact labels

[What does unverifiable mean in an AI product answer?](what-does-unverifiable-mean-in-an-ai-product-answer.md)

Unverifiable means the checked vendor pages did not settle the claim: 880 of 2,922 statements received that label in Wrong About You.
