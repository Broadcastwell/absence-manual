# Why does the buyer question matter to an AI shortlist?

A shortlist answers the need in the buyer's question, so a category total can conceal an absence on that particular need. On 22 September 2026, Google AI Overviews named Procore in 0 of 3 answers about construction software for a small residential builder, but in 21 of 30 answers across the ten construction questions. The larger total cannot stand in for the smaller question-level result.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The September Absence Index kept the engine, category and capture date fixed for this comparison. Its ten construction questions each had three scheduled answers. The small-residential-builder question supplied three of those thirty answers; it is part of the category total, rather than an independent comparison group.

Procore's 0 of 3 result has a descriptive 95% Wilson interval of 0.0% to 56.1%. Its 21 of 30 category result is 70.0%, with an interval of 52.1% to 83.3%. Buildertrend and Contractor Foreman were each named in all three answers to that specific question. These are counts of recorded names, with no ordering or product-quality score attached.

The [release and matching method](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695), published 23 September 2026, preserve the questions, three-run design and engine-level counts. **Reasoned.** The contrast shows why a reader needs the question beside the count. It does not isolate question wording as a causal treatment or establish that changing a website will change an answer.

## What this means for you

If you sell to residential builders, inspect that buyer condition before relying on a broad construction result. A need involving team size, work type or budget may deserve its own question in a measurement plan.

Keep a stable question set when comparing dates. Substituting an easier question changes what you measured, even if the headline category remains construction software.

Read a missing name as an observation about the captured answers. Three runs leave substantial uncertainty, and neither the absence nor the category total establishes whether Procore would suit a particular builder.
