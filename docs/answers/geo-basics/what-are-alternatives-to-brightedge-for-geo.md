# What are alternatives to BrightEdge for GEO?

Profound, Semrush and seoClarity publish functions to compare as alternatives to a BrightEdge GEO workflow: brand scoring, location-specific reports and Overview-reference inspection. Our [September Index](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) recorded 1496 checked-vendor name appearances across 420 ChatGPT answers on 22 September 2026, showing why a dashboard comparison needs to identify the unit behind its totals.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [Index data and method](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) span fourteen categories and ten questions per category, with three ChatGPT returns per question. We add each checked vendor's presence across those answers to obtain 1496 name appearances. That is not 1496 separate answers. Our panel does not compare these products' accuracy, contracts or commercial results.

**Reported.** Profound's Answer Engine Insights guide was read on 29 October 2026; it describes daily prompt-driven analysis and separate brand and citation fields. Semrush's toolkit guide, read on 30 October, distinguishes Brand Performance from Prompt Tracking. seoClarity's AIO guide was updated on 23 October 2025 and read on 29 October 2026, with filters and previews for Overview results.

**Reasoned.** Our 1496/420 example makes a brand-total comparison different from a count of captured answers. The documented field definitions matter when an alternative's report uses a term such as visibility or citations.

## What this means for you

Start with the reporting task. Profound defines a citation as a reference or link to a specific resource, while its Visibility score uses responses naming a brand. Its topics and tags organize the daily prompt dataset. If your BrightEdge workflow concerns linked product pages, the documented citation field addresses a different object from a combined vendor-name count.

Semrush separates domain-level Brand Performance from prompt monitoring. The former uses the location and language chosen during setup and updates weekly; its Prompt Tracking description names ChatGPT and Google AI Mode and updates daily. That gives a specific choice between a configured brand report and a report about the prompts you track.

seoClarity's dated guide addresses Overview inspection through its SERP Features filter. Its research scope is UK desktop keywords from September 2024 to September 2025. The tracking description includes whether a site or competitor is linked or referenced, with SERP Previews for reading the Overview content.
