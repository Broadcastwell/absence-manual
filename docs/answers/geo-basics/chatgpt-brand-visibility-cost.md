# What does tracking brand visibility in ChatGPT cost?

If you need a first brand check, Broadcastwell’s Free 10-question check costs $0; ongoing monitoring has a different scope. Specify the question set before comparing prices. Our [22 September 2026 CRM release method](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) (customer relationship management) counted Close in 3 of 30 ChatGPT answers. A quote for one answer would buy a different observation from that thirty-answer sample.

Observed answers on stated dates, not a ranking.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The CRM study asked ten buyer questions three times per engine. Its [published CRM CSV](https://index.broadcastwell.com/data/crm-2026-09.csv) records three ChatGPT naming observations for Close. The thirty-answer base includes the answers without that name; it is not thirty favorable mentions. Across all five engines, Close had twelve naming observations out of 150.

One [ChatGPT answer](https://zenodo.org/records/22907695/files/answers-2026-09.jsonl?download=1) captured on 22 September 2026 includes this wording:

> Pipedrive, HubSpot, Zoho CRM, and Close

The release does not record monitoring suppliers’ fees, staff time or the cost of repairing a company page. Its counts cannot establish a fair subscription price. They do show what must be specified when someone sells a number of prompts or tracked answers.

**Reported.** Broadcastwell's [Free 10-question check](https://audit.broadcastwell.com/) uses one engine, needs no email and has per-network and daily allowances. Its $0 entry scope is different from a supplier's recurring monitoring quote.

**Reasoned.** Ask a supplier whether one tracked question produces one answer per month, several repeats, or separate answers for several engines. Include the countries or languages, retained answer text, source links, exports and review work in the same written scope. An apparent price difference may purchase a different number of observations.

## What this means for you

The three Close observations all belong to the [small sales team that is scaling](https://index.broadcastwell.com/questions/crm/which-crm-options-work-well-for-a-small-sales-team-that-is-scaling/) question. Each ChatGPT repeat shortlisted Close alongside Pipedrive, HubSpot and Zoho. The other nine questions produced no Close naming observations on that engine. A service tracking only the scaling-team question would therefore be selling a narrower view than the thirty-answer panel, even though it could show the same three mentions.

The observation unit matters when you compare a correction with monitoring. In the Close answer's own comparison table, the source links include product pricing pages. A disputed price needs its billing unit and dated supplier source; a shortlist naming count does not establish what that correction would involve.
