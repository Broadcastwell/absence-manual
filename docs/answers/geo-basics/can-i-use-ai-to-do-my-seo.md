# Can I use AI to do my SEO?

Yes, AI can assist with SEO research and drafting, provided you check the factual material it supplies. Our [Wrong About You study](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23270124) labelled 2 of 47 HubSpot CRM statements wrong, using 22 September 2026 answer captures and an 8 October reference check. Those two statements concern free-tier contact limits, a concrete input that could otherwise enter a comparison page.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [published statement-review method and records](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23270124) separate forty-seven distinct HubSpot statements into thirty-one true, two wrong and fourteen unverifiable entries, with no stale entries. Both wrong entries are free-tier claims from Claude. One says the free CRM supports a million contacts; the other calls contacts unlimited. Their retained reference is HubSpot's CRM product page as read on 8 October. Each statement has observations across three returns, rather than becoming three separate statement entries. Team review: in progress. This study did not test SEO performance or a correction's effect on it.

**Reported.** Google's [AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), read on 29 October 2026, says established SEO practices remain relevant to AI Overviews and AI Mode. Its supporting-link requirements include indexing and permission to show a search snippet. OpenAI's [search help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-searching-the-web-with-chatgpt), read that day, describes web search with linked sources.

**Reasoned.** A linked answer can supply research material for an SEO draft; the retained HubSpot examples show why a plausible commercial detail still needs a dated reference.

## What this means for you

Treat the free-tier claim as an editorial dependency. A page comparing entry plans would change materially if it copied a million-contact allowance instead of the allowance preserved in the vendor reference. The error is in the input fact, before any decision about a title or internal link. A citation beside the generated sentence gives you somewhere to inspect; it does not replace that inspection.

Dates matter here. The answer captures precede the product-page check. If a new draft discusses today's terms, the October historical reference is evidence of the study's classification, rather than a substitute for reading today's terms. The two published entries also concern different propositions: a stated numerical ceiling and an unlimited allowance. Combining them into one loose statement about a generous plan would hide the precise disagreement.
