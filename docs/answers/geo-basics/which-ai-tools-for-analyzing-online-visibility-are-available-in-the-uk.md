# Which AI tools for analyzing online visibility are available in the UK?

Semrush documents United Kingdom coverage in its AI Visibility Toolkit, and seoClarity publishes UK AI Overview research. In our 22 September 2026 Index capture, Claude produced 2,039 checked-name appearances across 420 answers, showing why the engine and the count unit also belong in a UK tracking brief.

## What we measured

**Measured.** Our [September Index method and release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) checked software vendors across 14 categories, ten buyer questions per category and three runs per engine. Summing Claude's vendor-per-answer naming counts gives 2,039 appearances in its 420 answers; several checked vendors can appear in one answer. The Claude panel is not a UK sample and cannot rank these tools or establish account access.

**Reported.** The [Semrush toolkit guide](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-visibility-toolkit), read on 30 October 2026 UTC, explicitly includes the United Kingdom in Visibility Overview, Competitor Research and Prompt Research. The saved [seoClarity study](https://www.seoclarity.net/research/ai-overviews-impact), last updated 23 October 2025, includes UK desktop Overview observations from September 2024 to September 2025. **Reasoned.** Those are different reporting scopes and dates.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** For a UK brand comparison, Semrush's documented regional reports provide a named starting point. Visibility Overview can use worldwide or regional filtering. Brand Performance instead uses the location and language chosen during setup. Keep that choice beside the chart: a worldwide overview and a report configured for a particular UK location answer different geographical questions.

Semrush updates these reports at different intervals. Its guide says Visibility Overview, Competitor Research and Prompt Research update daily on a rolling basis; Brand Performance updates weekly, Prompt Tracking daily and AI Search Site Audit on demand. A new daily tracking result is therefore not evidence that the weekly brand report refreshed at the same time. Select the report that answers the question you intend to revisit.

The Claude total illustrates the other axis of that selection. The 2,039 count adds checked-vendor appearances, whereas 420 counts answers, so dividing one by the other describes appearances per answer rather than the share of answers containing your brand. Ask which engine and denominator a UK dashboard uses before comparing it with a percentage from another report. Neither a regional label nor a familiar score name makes those units interchangeable.

seoClarity's dated article concerns Google AI Overviews and describes an Overview filter in SERP Features, referenced-site checks and the returned content through SERP Previews. That is useful reported scope when your question concerns Google's source links, rather than Claude brand mentions. For Semrush account access, its toolkit FAQ specifies a paid subscription and an allocated limit for each corporate user; the generic site footer's trial invitation is separate from that toolkit's stated no-trial rule.
