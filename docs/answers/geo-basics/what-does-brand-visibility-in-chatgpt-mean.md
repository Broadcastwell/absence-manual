# What does brand visibility in ChatGPT mean?

Pipedrive was named in 17 of the 30 ChatGPT answers in our [22 September 2026 CRM panel](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) (customer relationship management). That is one bounded example of brand visibility: a checked name appearing in the answers a defined set of buyers might read. It is not a market-wide popularity score, a count of visits or a promise that your next question will return the same list.

Observed answers on stated dates, not a ranking.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The CRM panel used ten buyer questions, repeated three times on each of five engines. The ChatGPT slice therefore has thirty valid answers. The [release CSV](https://index.broadcastwell.com/data/crm-2026-09.csv) records Pipedrive’s ChatGPT naming count as 17; across all five engines, its naming count is 97 of 150.

One [ChatGPT answer](https://zenodo.org/records/22907695/files/answers-2026-09.jsonl?download=1) captured on 22 September 2026 includes this wording:

> HubSpot, Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Zoho CRM, and Pipedrive

The all-engine table separately records 29 of 150 answers that both name Pipedrive and cite its owned domain. Those twenty-nine observations use a different measure from the seventeen ChatGPT names. The retained cited-host list contains pipedrive.com and support.pipedrive.com. A name can appear without a link.

**Reported.** [OpenAI’s search documentation](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-searching-the-web-with-chatgpt), read on 29 October 2026, describes web results with linked sources and explains that local context can affect searches. Our published counts concern the saved September answers, not every current configuration or user.

**Reasoned.** Start your own report by defining whether visibility means a name, an explicit recommendation or a cited source. Our panel cannot establish Pipedrive’s sales, buyer preference or present-day share of all ChatGPT use.

## What this means for you

If you sell CRM software, compare the exact question with the product decision you care about. Pipedrive appeared somewhere in nine of the panel’s ten questions when all engines were combined. It had no recorded name in the question asking what a buyer should evaluate in a CRM. The reporting-and-forecasting question named it in thirteen of fifteen cross-engine answers, while all fifteen spreadsheet-replacement answers named it. Those two results would disappear inside a single average.

Do not call the combined fifteen-answer result a ChatGPT rate. It pools five engines. Keep the thirty-answer ChatGPT result in its own column, and keep a complete answer beside any recommendation label so a reader can see whether the tool was endorsed, mentioned as an alternative or used in a qualification.
