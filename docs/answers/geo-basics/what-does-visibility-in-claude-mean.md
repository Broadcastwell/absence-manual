# What does visibility in Claude mean?

Visibility in Claude means that a defined set of Claude answers actually contains your company, recommendation or source link, according to the measure you chose. In our [CRM capture of 22 September 2026](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) (customer relationship management), Freshsales appeared by name in 8 of 30 Claude answers. The product name and the cited host require separate checks.

Observed answers on stated dates, not a ranking.

## What we measured

**Measured.** Our [CRM release data](https://index.broadcastwell.com/data/crm-2026-09.csv) distinguishes the Freshsales product name from its canonical domain, freshworks.com. The eight Claude names came from four buyer questions: the complex-sales-pipeline question contributed two, the traditional-CRM alternatives question one, the scaling-team question two and the spreadsheet-replacement question three. Each question had three Claude returns.

One [Claude answer](https://zenodo.org/records/22907695/files/answers-2026-09.jsonl?download=1) captured on 22 September 2026 includes this wording:

> for bootstrapped or cost-sensitive organizations, Zoho or Freshsales

The remaining six Claude questions supplied no Freshsales name. Across the entire five-engine panel, Freshsales was named in 22 of 150 answers and appeared somewhere in six of the ten questions. Neither number should replace the engine-specific 8/30 result in a Claude report.

**Reported.** [Anthropic’s crawler guidance](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8896518-does-anthropic-crawl-data-from-the-web-and-how-can-site-owners-block-the-crawler), read on 29 October 2026, describes separate bot roles and publisher controls. A crawler’s permitted access is a different observation from one of our saved answers naming Freshsales.

**Reasoned.** Use the answer and its cited address to distinguish a product mention from a visit to a publisher’s site. This capture cannot tell you whether a buyer later clicked, selected Freshsales or opened a sales inquiry.

## What this means for you

Decide which Freshsales question you want the report to answer. The spreadsheet-replacement result was three of three Claude returns; the traditional-CRM alternatives result was one of three. A single favorable screenshot could describe the former while concealing the latter. Keep the other repeats, including the answers with no name.

Use the right product label when you inspect source links. An answer can write “Freshsales” while referring to a Freshworks page. Looking only for the word Freshsales inside URLs would confuse product naming with domain citation. The CSV’s zero citation count means that no answer both named the checked Freshsales product and cited its owned domain. A raw Freshworks sales-page URL is nevertheless present in one Google AI Overviews answer to the question about a business-to-business (B2B) sales team, without that checked product name. Freshsales is the checked product.

When you compare engines, keep the same questions and dates. The release records zero Freshsales names in thirty ChatGPT answers, alongside Claude’s eight. That comparison is an observed difference under the saved method, not evidence that changing a bot rule would move one engine’s result toward the other.
