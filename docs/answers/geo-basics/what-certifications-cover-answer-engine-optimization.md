# What certifications cover answer engine optimization?

On 30 October 2026, HubSpot Academy’s public [AEO certification page](https://offers.hubspot.com/academy-aeo-certifications) lists AEO Fundamentals and Implementing AEO With HubSpot. Read the credential’s scope beside an actual evidence task: our [Wrong About You study](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23270124) labelled 3 of 32 Contractor Foreman statements wrong, comparing 22 September 2026 answers with a vendor reference checked 8 October 2026. That historical fact-checking result is not a score for either certification.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [study method](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23270124) classifies retained product statements against dated public vendor references. Contractor Foreman has 29 true and 3 wrong statements from a 32-statement base. One error treats a company fee as a per-user charge; another gives the wrong user allowance; the third describes a free tier. The public team review remains in progress. The study did not measure course completion or credential holders’ results.

**Reported.** [HubSpot’s certification overview](https://offers.hubspot.com/academy-aeo-certifications) labels Fundamentals free and product-agnostic, and the implementation certification free and HubSpot-specific. The [implementation course page](https://academy.hubspot.com/courses/implementing-aeo-with-hubspot-certification-en) lists seven lessons, 23 videos and four practical exercises, with a published duration of 2 hours 58 minutes.

**Reasoned.** A named educational credential tells you which programme a person completed. A source-checking example tells you how a particular claim was assessed. Keep the two records distinct.

## What this means for you

Choose between the advertised scopes. The Fundamentals description concerns an AEO strategy and measurement vocabulary; the implementation programme concerns using HubSpot’s tools. A certificate carrying the latter title should not be presented as evidence of practical experience on every other platform.

If you are assessing someone’s work, ask them to distinguish the pricing unit in the Contractor Foreman example. In the historical reference, the listed fee applies to the company rather than each user. Check the unit.

Keep the reference date attached when you use that exercise. The 8 October comparison concerns what the vendor’s page stated then; it is not a current subscription quotation. A useful answer would identify the billing and user assumptions and point to the relevant dated passage instead of repeating the model’s per-user wording.
