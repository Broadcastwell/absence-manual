# How do Profound and SEO Clarity compare for tracking brand coverage in AI answers?

If you are comparing Profound with seoClarity, compare the definitions and answer records before choosing a score. Profound publishes distinct visibility and share-of-voice denominators; seoClarity describes separate mention and citation tracking. In our CRM capture on 22 September 2026, HubSpot CRM had 113 naming answers and 34 answers with an own-domain citation, each from 150 valid answers. Those two events give you a concrete test case for reading the definitions.

## What we measured

**Reported.** [Profound's metric guide](https://docs.tryprofound.com/answer-engine-insights/about), read on 29 October 2026, defines visibility over responses containing at least one brand, and share of voice over total brand mentions. [seoClarity's ArcAI visibility page](https://www.seoclarity.net/ai-seo/ai-search-engine-visibility), read the same day, describes tracking both mentions and citations, including separate Google AI Overview and AI Mode views.

**Measured.** HubSpot CRM's figures come from the [September Index](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695), with ten CRM questions and three runs on each of five engines. The naming interval is 67.9% to 81.5%; the own-domain citation interval is 16.7% to 30.0%, both at 95% confidence. The base of 150 includes every valid CRM answer.

Our Index observations cannot establish which of these tracking products performs better.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Make the terms match. For Profound, the published distinction between a response base and a brand-mention base is the first comparison to resolve. For ArcAI, retain its separate mention and citation fields when asking how a chart was counted. Neither product should inherit our 113 or 34 as its expected result.

For the HubSpot CRM example, ask how a provider would represent a name without an own-domain citation. Then inspect the cited URL when the citation field is positive. An article about HubSpot on another publisher's domain belongs to a different field from a citation to HubSpot's own site.

If your decision concerns Google AI Overviews, ask for that exact surface's question set and retained-answer base rather than substituting an AI Mode chart. ArcAI's own product page separates the two experiences. That public distinction gives you a useful requirement to put into the comparison.

For an agency handover, put the two providers' definitions beside the particular export fields you inspected. A short list of unresolved differences, such as the treatment of responses containing no brand, gives your client something concrete to decide before a subscription or delivery scope is selected.
