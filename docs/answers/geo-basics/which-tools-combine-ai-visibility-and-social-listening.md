# Which tools combine AI visibility and social listening?

Meltwater's [GenAI Lens](https://www.meltwater.com/en/capabilities/ai-visibility-tracking) is one documented example of AI visibility alongside social listening in a broader platform. Our [22 September 2026 Index](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) recorded Reddit URLs in 60 of 150 radiology-software answers. That count concerns links in AI answers, a different observation from the social conversations a listening product collects.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [original Index method and data](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) retain ten radiology buyer questions, three returns and five engines. We counted an answer once when its recorded source URLs included reddit.com. The result is sixty citing answers, without a count of unique posts, authors or social conversations. This panel did not test Meltwater's coverage or its product performance.

**Reported.** Meltwater's capability page, read on 30 October 2026, places GenAI Lens alongside news monitoring and social listening, as a modular add-on in the existing platform. Its [official documentation](https://community.meltwater.com/meltwater-product-answers-428/meltwater-genai-lens-11603) describes dated prompt-response records, cited links and package-dependent model coverage.

**Reasoned.** The recorded Reddit links make the distinction between these two streams concrete: an AI answer's citation can concern a social platform without being a direct observation of that platform's conversations.

## What this means for you

Meltwater's public product material describes the combined platform rather than a requirement to stitch unrelated products together. The capability page specifically says the add-on appears alongside existing monitoring, social listening and analytics. That documented packaging is the named route relevant to this question, rather than a claim that every current Meltwater subscription contains it.

Inspect the export fields. The documentation describes an Overview grid with model, prompt, date and links. Its Excel export separates the selected date range, models and prompts from detailed results. It also says automatically generated prompt variations count toward the subscription allowance. Those fields let a buyer preserve the prompt and collection scope of an AI record instead of treating every displayed row as another social conversation.
