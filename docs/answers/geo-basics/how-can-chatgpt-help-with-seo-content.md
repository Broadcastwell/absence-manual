# How can ChatGPT help with SEO content?

4 of 30 JobTread statements were labeled wrong in our [Wrong About You study](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23270124), which compared the 22 September 2026 capture with a vendor reference read on 8 October. Three concerned pricing and one concerned a free trial. Give ChatGPT a bounded writing job, then check the product facts before you publish the result. Fluent copy can carry a specific buying error into an otherwise useful SEO (search engine optimization) page.

Observed answers on stated dates, not a ranking.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [study method](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23270124) classifies statements against dated vendor references. Its [JobTread record](https://index.broadcastwell.com/wrong-about-you/) separates 25 statements labeled true, four wrong and one unverifiable. These are classified statements, rather than thirty answers or thirty website pages. Its retained Claude answer for a small general contractor described a $199 annual-billing starting price; another assigned JobTread an approximately $99 monthly entry point.

The retained historical Claude [statement](https://index.broadcastwell.com/vendors/jobtread/construction-project-management/#wrong-about-you), captured on 22 September 2026 and marked wrong against the dated vendor reference, reads:

> Pricing starts at $199/month billed annually for one internal user.

This is the disputed historical statement, not a current vendor price. Team review: in progress.

The study checked the vendor’s public reference on 8 October 2026. That reference separated the $199 monthly platform charge from $20 per internal user and described an annual-subscription discount. The figures here describe that historical comparison, not today’s quote. The public review label remains “Team review: in progress”. The study did not test whether using ChatGPT improved a page’s search performance.

**Reported.** [OpenAI’s search help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-searching-the-web-with-chatgpt), read on 29 October 2026, explains that ChatGPT can retrieve current web information and link to sources. Search access does not make a copied price correct. [Google’s AI-feature guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), read on 29 October 2026, retains the usual search requirements and does not prescribe special AI markup.

**Reasoned.** Use the assistant for a first draft, a comparison of supplied passages or a list of questions left unanswered by a source. Treat its additional product claims as material to check.

## What this means for you

For a JobTread comparison page, supply the dated pricing passage and ask for separate fields for the platform charge, user charge and billing period. A heading saying “annual price” should not inherit a monthly number merely because the model placed the two phrases together. Read the vendor’s page yourself before turning the historical study example into a current offer. Check the billing period.

You can also ask for an outline answering the small-contractor question already present in the study: estimating and scheduling without Procore complexity. Have a person select the comparison criteria from real product documents. If the draft adds a free-trial recommendation, check it separately: that was one of the JobTread statements the study marked wrong.
