## What this means for you

Read the cell. WebFX's B2B cases status is Yes under a criterion about published named software-client material. Its Machine readable status is also Yes, under a criterion concerning buyer-readable account, export or connector material. Neither cell is a measured league position for work in a competitive industry.

The two documentation fields also stay separate. Method is Partial, while Open data is No. The scorecard's Method rule asks for a published method and at least one complete public question set; its Open data rule asks for a DOI. A general process description and a downloadable answer collection would therefore answer different parts of this checklist. Your procurement record can preserve the actual status of each field rather than substituting a single trust score.

For the Koomba case, inspect the union in its visibility definition. A source citation can satisfy the case's named-or-cited condition even when a naming-only test would not. The stated launch is in July, before both of its displayed August and September windows. Those calendar labels matter when reading the particular case; the case does not supply matched observations for WebFX or every other agency in the scorecard.
