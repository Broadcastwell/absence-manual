# What evidence supports a page's selection as a source in an AI answer?

Capterra.com was a cited host in 34 of 150 construction-software answers in our [September Index](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695), collected on 22 September 2026 across five engines. That supports a host-level citation observation; explaining a particular page’s selection requires its returned URL and answer, and still does not reveal the engine’s internal reason.

## What we measured

**Measured.** Under the [Index citation method](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695), the construction category pools ten buyer questions, three repeats and five engines. A host is counted once per answer in which it appears. The thirty-four host appearances do not identify thirty-four distinct Capterra pages, nor establish that every engine selected the same URL.

**Reported.** Google's [Search Central guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), read on 29 October 2026, says a supporting page must be indexed and eligible for a Search snippet. It also says that satisfying requirements does not guarantee indexing or serving. Eligibility and an observed selection are therefore different claims, even within Google's own named features.

**Reasoned.** The returned citation is direct evidence that an answer linked a source. The source page can then be read to check what it actually supports. Neither the presence of the link nor a generally eligible page establishes which sentence, formatting choice or reputation signal caused the selection.

## What this means for you

Read the host names. The construction export keeps capterra.com, g2.com and getapp.com separate, with 34, 21 and six appearances respectively in the same 150-answer base. A Capterra host total cannot be converted into the URL of a particular review page. It also cannot absorb the G2 or GetApp observations simply because all three publish software information.

Procore's own record illustrates a second distinction in that export. It has 109 named appearances but 30 own-domain citations across the pooled 150 construction answers, and its cited-domain list names procore.com and learn.procore.com. The domains identify a vendor source family; the differing name and citation counts show why a brand-mention record cannot stand in for a returned page link. An assertion about one linked document still needs that document's URL, not an invented page under either host.
