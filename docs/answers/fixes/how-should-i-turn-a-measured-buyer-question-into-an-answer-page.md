# How should I turn a measured buyer question into an answer page?

Turn the measured question into a page about the same buyer decision, then show what your evidence can answer. In construction answers captured on 22 September 2026, Study 7 recorded workflow fit in 17 of 149 eligible answers. That finding supplies a concrete topic to investigate, not proof that publishing a workflow page will change an AI answer.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [published Study 7 record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23271935) connects construction criteria to the answer spans from which they were extracted. Workflow fit was one of 92 criteria in that category. Its frequency of 17 in 149 answers is 11.4 percent, with a descriptive Wilson 95 percent interval from 7.2 to 17.5 percent.

Criteria were extracted before the first vendor name. One request-limit answer was excluded from this derived collection; its base therefore differs from the original 150-answer Index category. Coding review remains in progress. The method describes AI-stated criteria and dated vendor-page comparisons, not an experiment on a new answer page.

**Reasoned.** Preserve the buyer task before choosing a title or product example. A criterion observed across answers may help you decide what evidence to gather, but it cannot establish that your product meets the criterion.

## What this means for you

Write an answer outline with three records beside it: the decision being answered, the observation that justified examining it, and the product evidence you can publish. Keep these records distinct. The measurement can show that workflow fit was mentioned; a product document must establish the workflow your page describes.

Open with the answer your sources support. Explain which users, work and product scope it covers. If the evidence settles only one part of the buyer's question, say which part remains unresolved instead of expanding the opening into a universal recommendation.

Develop the page around the steps a reader needs to judge that answer. A workflow topic might require the task, supported path, prerequisites and boundaries. Link each factual step to the relevant source. Include only the context the reader needs; private personal or client details from a question collection do not belong in the public answer.

Before adding a URL, inspect the titles and purposes of existing pages. Extend an existing answer when it already resolves the same question. Otherwise, make the new page's distinct decision clear and link to broader guides for context. After publication, check the served text and links. That verifies delivery of the answer; it leaves any effect on later AI output unmeasured.

- **For a product team:** establish the product facts before writing the answer.
- **For an editor:** keep one decision, one supported scope and visible evidence boundaries.
