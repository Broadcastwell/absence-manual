# What should a GEO content and implementation scope include?

A GEO content and implementation scope needs the pages, supported claims, proposed edits and delivery checks you are buying. Procore's dated page review on 8 October 2026 recorded 20 stated, 44 unstated and 28 unknown criteria out of 92. A research gap in that table starts an investigation; it does not automatically become an approved product claim or a completed page change.

## What we measured

**Measured.** [Study 7](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23271935) connects extracted answer criteria with dated page evidence. Its full method covered 2,003 criteria from 2,093 eligible September answers across 14 categories. The construction captures were on 22 September; the Procore page comparison on 8 October produced the 20/44/28 source states across 92 criteria. These are AI-stated requirements and page classifications, not independently tested features. Team review remains in progress, with no independently verified criteria in the published review state.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Give the proposal a change list that a reader can assess. For each page, identify the reader question, the factual statement to add or correct, and the primary evidence needed before approval. Keep unsupported ideas in a research list rather than presenting them as copy that is ready to publish.

Describe implementation separately from writing. Does the work end with a draft, an approved edit in the site system, or a served page that has been checked? Name any required template or navigation work and the evidence that shows the intended text is visible. A document containing proposed changes is a useful output, but it does not establish delivery on the site.

Use the Procore example to set the approval boundary. An unstated criterion needs a matching product source before you can fill the gap. An unknown row may need a narrower claim or a question back to the product owner. Neither is permission to invent a feature because an AI answer mentioned it.

Keep the acceptance test tied to the edit: supported wording, correct scope, working source link and served result. A later change in AI naming is a separate observation, not the evidence that the agreed page work was completed.

- **For a buyer:** identify the exact source-backed edit and the delivered form you will accept.
- **For an agency:** separate research, approval and implementation in the scope and status record.
- **For a vendor:** resolve product truth before approving a page claim.
