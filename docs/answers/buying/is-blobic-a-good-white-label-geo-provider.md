# Is Blobic a good white label GEO provider?

Blobic publishes a concrete white-label scope, including branded audits, monthly reports and a promise to leave the client relationship with your agency. That makes it a candidate you can examine, without establishing its delivery quality. In Wrong About You's 22 and 23 September 2026 captures, 118 of 2,042 verifiable statements were wrong or stale. For a sample white-label audit, you can examine whether the cited product evidence supports each factual claim.

## What we measured

**Reported.** Blobic's [white-label page](https://www.blobic.com/white-label-aeo/), read on 29 October 2026, describes a prompt map, visibility baseline, source graph and prioritised roadmap. It reports monthly measurement and white-label presentation, with confidentiality and non-circumvention terms. These are supplier statements, without an observed delivery test here.

**Measured.** The [Wrong About You protocol](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23270124) classified statements about 70 software vendors from the September answers against their public product pages read on 8 October. Of 2,042 verifiable statements, 113 were coded wrong and five stale. The separate 880 unverifiable statements are outside that base. Team review of flagged rows remains in progress.

The statement audit does not establish the accuracy or commercial results of Blobic's reports.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Read a sample before choosing. Blobic's promised baseline includes mentions, recommendations and citations. Ask for the definitions beside those columns so your account team can explain whether each value records a name, a source link or an expressed preference.

Our statement example gives that review a specific test. A flagged Procore answer said it had no pricing page; the saved product source contained a pricing page. Another flagged statement described per-user pricing. A sample white-label report should let your client open the relevant product evidence for an equally concrete claim instead of inheriting a confident sentence without its receipt.

Blobic's short methodology page describes repeated prompt tests and later remeasurement. The inspected page does not provide a complete fixed question list, the exclusion rules or an uncertainty protocol. Request those elements for the proposed client scope so an initial baseline and a later report can be compared on the same basis.

Its delivery terms need their own check. The white-label page describes a standard audit taking two to three weeks, native English and Spanish work, and other languages on request. If your client has an English report due sooner, that published turnaround leaves a specific scheduling question for the proposal.

Finally, put Blobic's no-direct-client-contact promise beside the actual reporting and presentation arrangements. The agency remains the named owner of the relationship in the published scope. Ask for the sample's branding, correction process and evidence handover to reflect that arrangement before you give the account team a delivery date.
