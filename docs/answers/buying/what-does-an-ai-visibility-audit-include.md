# What does an AI visibility audit include?

Three Yes fields appear in [Go Fish Digital](https://broadcastwell.com/best-geo-agencies#go-fish-digital)'s 3/8 publication record: Engines, B2B cases and Reviews. An AI visibility audit should include the observed answers, questions, engines, run dates and cited sources. Our source copy was read on 29 October 2026. Its Method entry is Partial and Machine readable is No. You need the actual evidence handover specified alongside any list of covered engines.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Ask for the question set before discussing a headline visibility score. The Method Partial entry means a method description did not meet the complete public-question-set criterion. For your own audit, the handover should let a colleague identify the exact buyer question behind a recorded answer, rather than infer the question from a chart label.

Keep the engine label with the response. Go Fish's Engines Yes concerns named coverage in its published material; it does not itself contain the answers from your proposed engagement. An engine list in a proposal and a dated response from that engine are different items. The response record is what allows you to inspect a named competitor or a missing company.

Keep the losses too. Our [audit-handover guidance](https://broadcastwell.com/best-ai-visibility-audit-services) calls for the returned text, engine, time and cited sources for every observation. An absent company in a valid answer differs from a failed or excluded response. Without the exclusion reason, a reviewer cannot reconstruct the denominator used for a percentage.

Specify the export. Machine readable No in this public record is a reason to settle the delivery format explicitly. A chart image is hard to reconcile with individual answers; a table can retain question, engine, run, response and source links as separate fields. That is a requested deliverable, not a claim that we tested the supplier's private reporting account.

Keep buying terms beside the evidence scope. Price No and Refund No here describe the dated publication criteria, not the terms of a quote you have received. Before an audit is commissioned, attach its actual scope and applicable terms to the same work order that names the response export.
