# Should I buy AI visibility monitoring or a completed page fix?

Buy monitoring when you need dated observations; buy a page fix when you can name the source change and its acceptance check. On a Procore page read on 8 October 2026, Study 7 recorded 20 stated, 44 unstated and 28 unknown criteria out of 92. That table can help identify a question to investigate, but it does not turn every unstated criterion into work the product needs.

## What we measured

**Measured.** [Study 7](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23271935) supplies a page-evidence example for this delivery decision. Against 92 AI-stated construction criteria, the Procore page read on 8 October 2026 had 20 stated, 44 not stated and 28 unknown. Construction answers were captured on 22 September. The method compares a dated page with extracted criteria; it does not test the product or establish which omissions need a paid change. The criteria have not been independently verified and team review remains in progress.

The separate [Wrong About You method](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23270124) assessed claims in 2,922 statements about 70 vendors from 22 and 23 September answers against pages read on 8 October. Its 118 wrong or stale labels and 880 unverifiable labels distinguish a contradiction from an evidence gap. Team review remains in progress. These are source-review records, not measured results for a monitoring or implementation supplier.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Put the deliverable in the purchase decision. A monitoring engagement should return observations you can inspect: dates, questions, engines, answers and clear field definitions. A completed change should name the page, the supported fact, the edit and the check that demonstrates the edit is served.

For the Procore example, an unstated criterion first calls for a product-source check. If an authoritative source confirms the feature, a clearer page may be useful. If the answer remains unknown, publishing a confident feature claim would create another evidence problem. The separate accuracy study shows why unsupported and false statements need different treatment.

A supplier may provide both observation and implementation. Ask which is in the agreed scope and what evidence ends each piece of work. A new dashboard entry is evidence of observation; a served, checked page is evidence of delivery. Neither alone demonstrates that later AI answers improved.

- **If you buy the work:** specify the observation file or served page you expect to receive.
- **If you are an agency:** assign responsibility for approving product facts before anyone edits client copy.
- **If you sell software:** resolve the product truth before treating a page omission as a feature gap.
