# Does Althea Labs deliver faster AI visibility results than Keenfolks or SourceCode Communications?

An agency list cannot settle which of these firms delivers faster results. Our [17 September 2026 audit](https://broadcastwell.com/best-geo-agencies-lists-audited) found 29/32 agency-list pages included their own publisher. Compare Althea Labs, Keenfolks and SourceCode Communications using the event each timeline actually describes, rather than a position on a list.

## What we measured

**Measured.** Our [published list audit](https://broadcastwell.com/best-geo-agencies-lists-audited) records 32 pages, counted once per page. Its [dated row receipt](https://docs.broadcastwell.com/assets/library/agency-list-audit-2026-09-17.json) keeps 29 Yes entries, two No entries and one Parent company listed entry separate. The [publication criteria](https://broadcastwell.com/best-geo-agencies-lists-audited#how-scored) cover disclosures, selection criteria, claim sources, dates, prices and corrections. Broadcastwell is the publisher and is outside its scored table. The audit does not measure client outcomes or service quality.

**Reported.** Althea's [services](https://althealabs.co/services) and [FAQ](https://althealabs.co/faq), read on 30 October 2026, describe a five-day Brand Intelligence Audit and a three-to-six-month AI Visibility Sprint. Its FAQ presents 60 to 90 days as its experience of early signs, including site changes, first placements and community presence; movement in AI answers is described over subsequent months. A [13 May 2026 geoSurge announcement](https://geosurge.ai/posts/geosurge-joins-keenfolks-x-as-a-founding-technology-partner) identifies its technology partnership with Keenfolks X_. SourceCode's [service page](https://sourcecodecommunications.com/challenges-we-solve/), read on 30 October, describes AI-answer analysis and content playbooks.

**Reasoned.** A deck delivery, an engagement length and an estimate for early signs are three different endpoints. Treating them as one stopwatch would erase the distinction in Althea's own descriptions.

## What this means for you

Name the timed deliverable. Althea says its five-day audit arrives as a deck and a one-hour session, with recommendations for the following 30 to 45 days. That gives you a concrete document-and-meeting endpoint to put in a comparison. The FAQ's 60-to-90-day discussion concerns different work: site changes, placements and community presence, followed by movement in answers over later months.

For Keenfolks, the saved geoSurge article establishes a named partnership, dated 13 May, within the Keenfolks X_ network. It describes geoSurge's AI-visibility role alongside the network's broader strategy, data, technology and execution. A partner announcement belongs in the provider-identity record; a delivery period would need to describe the particular work your comparison is timing. The reviewed announcement supplies the relationship and its date.

SourceCode describes examining how a brand and its competitors appear in Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT, then producing playbooks about content, formats, sources and narratives. In a proposal comparison, give that analysis-and-playbook output its own delivery field. Its reviewed service description names those two engine examples and the content-planning output; the five-day deck-and-session period above belongs specifically to Althea's audit.
