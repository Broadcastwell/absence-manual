# Looking for AI software for field service providers in the United Kingdom?

Jobber is an option to examine: its help updated on 28 October 2026 places Receptionist in the United Kingdom, with separate website-chat and phone/text conditions. We counted Jobber in 18 of 30 ChatGPT field-service software answers captured on 22 September 2026. Use the product guides to check what that observed name would mean for your UK business.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [September Index method and release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) used ten fixed field-service buyer questions with three ChatGPT runs apiece. Jobber appeared in 18 of those 30 answers. Each vendor is counted once per answer, even if the response names it again. This count does not establish UK account access, feature performance or completed bookings.

**Reported.** Jobber's [Website guide](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/website-marketing-tools/) and [Dedicated Phone Number guide](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/dedicated-phone-number/), updated 28 October and read on 30 October 2026 UTC, explicitly name the United Kingdom for the documented Receptionist and phone-number routes. The website article lists Receptionist chat with all plans, while phone and text replies depend on an applicable add-on or Plus plan. Its website-publication instructions also require an active supported subscription.

**Reasoned.** Those conditions belong beside the telephone feature in a buying brief, rather than being inferred from the website-chat listing.

## What this means for you

Choose the contact channel. Jobber describes website chat that can answer questions, submit service requests and book jobs when Online Booking is set up. For a UK heating business, a visitor asking about boiler servicing could produce a service request; direct booking depends on that separate booking configuration. The guide names both operations, so the wording of the website button matters.

For phone and text, Jobber says a dedicated phone number is still required even when you continue using an existing business number. Its number guide lists UK support on selected plans, excludes number setup during a trial and reserves setup for administrators. The same guide says a selected dedicated number cannot be changed.

Check the role that will maintain the service. Jobber limits the Receptionist dashboard and settings to administrators. Its website instructions allow administrators or users with Marketing Suite permission to generate and publish the site. A staff member able to edit the marketing website may therefore have a different role from the person managing Receptionist settings.
