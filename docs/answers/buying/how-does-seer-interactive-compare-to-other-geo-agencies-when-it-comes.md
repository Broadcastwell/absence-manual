# How does Seer Interactive compare to other GEO agencies when it comes to research, measurement capabilities, and proven client results?

On our 29 October 2026 read, Seer Interactive publishes original research and named measurement deliverables, but the inspected material does not support an agency-wide verdict on superior client results. Compare what each study measures: in our Head to Head capture of 22 September 2026, 75 of 359 scored answers chose one vendor. That is an answer-choice result, rather than a test of Seer or another agency's delivery.

## What we measured

**Reported.** Seer's [GEO service page](https://www.seerinteractive.com/generative-engine-optimization), read on 29 October 2026, lists a prompt-tracking plan, citation/source analysis, log-file analysis and a structured test framework. Its linked June fan-out research describes a 617-prompt analysis and one prompt repeated 30 times. Those study components concern search-query behaviour; they are not matched agency client-outcome groups.

**Measured.** Our [Head to Head protocol](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23202939) read comparison answers twice and resolved disagreements through a third read. Across 24 comparison questions, 359 answers were scored and one target was unscored. Seventy-five scored answers picked a single vendor; the remaining classifications retained both, neither or depends.

Neither that result nor a published research method establishes Seer's superiority in a client engagement.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Compare the research unit. Seer's June analysis examines query fan-outs and includes a repeat-prompt example. Our 359-answer result examines expressed choices between named software products. Putting the two sample sizes into one largest-study column would lose the event each research design counted.

For the proposed service, Seer's deliverable list is more useful than a broad claim to do GEO. If your concern is a wrong citation, ask how Citation + Source Analysis retains the answer and source passage. If you need an access investigation, its listed AI Log File Analysis is a different deliverable and needs the applicable site records.

Its Structured Test Framework also needs a concrete brief. The Head to Head example has a target set, an unscored record and explicit outcome categories. Ask Seer and the other agencies on your shortlist for equivalent definitions in their proposed tests, including what happens when an answer supplies no usable result.

Read client evidence separately from those methods. The inspected outdoor-living case does not name the client or place an absolute baseline beside its headline citation-growth figure. Its time descriptions also differ within the page. Those limits leave that case unsuitable as a matched performance benchmark against another agency's engagement.
