# Which agencies document work across AI answer engines?

Our [Go Fish Digital comparison record](https://broadcastwell.com/best-geo-agencies#go-fish-digital) documents a named GEO service and an Engines Yes, with 3/8 Yes cells in the saved page we read on 29 October 2026. Its engine list, rechecked on 28 October, names Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, ChatGPT and Bing Copilot. That makes it a documented example to inspect, rather than proof of successful work on every AI engine.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Start with the named surfaces. Google AI Mode and Google AI Overviews are separate entries in this record. If your brief requires both, preserve both names in the proposed scope rather than accepting Google as a sufficient engine label. Claude and Perplexity should remain questions for the provider; this particular saved list cannot supply them by implication.

The Method cell is Partial despite the Engines Yes. A list of platforms answers where an agency says it works, while the method criterion asks what a reader can check about the measurement. Ask for the method document that would govern your specific question set and retained answers.

The Open data No matters for a handover. It describes the absence of qualifying published data in this comparison, not a finding that the agency has no internal data. If you need the raw observations as a deliverable, put that requirement in the scope instead of assuming it follows from a case-study link.

Read the case evidence separately. The B2B cases Yes means published case material met that criterion; it is not an independent reproduction of the client's result.

For a buyer looking at the commercial fields, Price and Refund are both No in this dated record. Those cells provide no published service price or qualifying refund term to carry into your budget. Keep the exact engine coverage alongside the unanswered commercial questions when comparing a proposal.
