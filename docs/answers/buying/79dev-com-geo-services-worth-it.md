# 79dev.com GEO services worth it?

If you are considering 79 Development, judge its published audit and implementation scope against the evidence your business needs. Our [Volume II study](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21586091) found 25 of 85 companies were cited more often than named on 18 and 23 July 2026, so mentions and citations should remain separate in that buying decision.

## What we measured

**Reported.** The [79 Development site](https://79dev.com), read on 29 October 2026, describes an AEO audit with repeated prompt runs, competitor comparisons, separate mentions and citations, and a live sixty-minute readout. It also describes a one-time Foundation implementation, ongoing Authority work and Strategic Advisory in which the buyer's team implements between sessions. Those are different delivery responsibilities, not interchangeable names for an audit.

**Measured.** The [Volume II study design](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21586091) covers 85 companies and separates name appearances from citations to a company's own domain. Twenty-five companies had more own-domain citations than name appearances. A cited source may contribute to an answer without the answer presenting that company as the buyer's choice.

**Reasoned.** Broadcastwell publishes competing services and is not scored here. Separating company names from own-domain citations gives you a concrete audit requirement to check against 79 Development's description. Our sample does not establish the value, performance or return of a contract with that provider.

## What this means for you

Match the work to its owner. Under the published Strategic Advisory description, your team executes between working sessions. Under Foundation, the provider describes building and handing over the system. An audit readout alone should not be treated as an agreement to implement the recommendations.

For the audit itself, retain its proposed prompt set and ask how the separate mention and citation fields appear in the delivered report. Volume II's 25 cases explain why a single combined “visibility” total could conceal a difference that matters to the buyer. A citation to an explanatory article and a named recommendation should remain identifiable as different events.

79 Development's site describes the audit as credited toward Foundation or Authority within sixty days. That published condition links its diagnostic work to two named implementation offers. Check whether the same sixty-day credit appears in the written Foundation or Authority scope you receive; a link to the audit page alone does not settle the terms of that later work.
