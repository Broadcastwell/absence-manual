# Which AEO tools suit home-service companies and contractors?

For your home-service company, compare a location-aware brand report with the questions it can track: [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-visibility-toolkit) documents both, and [seoClarity](https://www.seoclarity.net/research/ai-overviews-impact) describes Overview-reference checks. Our [Index](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) found Jobber in 18 answers from a 30-return ChatGPT panel on 22 September 2026; those questions concern field-service software.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [September Index method](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) uses ten field-service software questions, with three ChatGPT returns per question. Jobber's checked name appears in 18 of those 30 answers. This software-buyer panel does not establish a local contractor's visibility or test an AEO tool.

**Reported.** Semrush's guide, read on 30 October 2026, describes Brand Performance reports centered on one domain and a selected location and language. Those reports update weekly. Its separate Prompt Tracking description names ChatGPT and Google AI Mode, with daily updates. seoClarity's guide was updated on 23 October 2025 and read on 29 October 2026; it describes AIO keyword filters, linked-domain checks and SERP Previews.

**Reasoned.** A contractor comparing tools needs to distinguish software-selection questions from questions that could name the contractor itself. The Jobber count belongs to the former. A domain, location and question set identify which business a proposed visibility report actually concerns.

## What this means for you

Name the business. Jobber is the software vendor being counted in our panel, rather than a plumbing, roofing or heating business recommended to a household. If you want to inspect your own service company's presence, the company and service area in the tracked question must reflect that purpose. A field-service software ranking supplies a different observation.

Semrush's report settings address part of that local scope. Its published description ties Brand Performance to the domain, location and language selected during setup, including city-level choices. A weekly report on that configured business is a different cadence from the daily Prompt Tracking report. Those are specific report choices to compare when your contractor has a defined service area.

Keep the Google surfaces distinct. The saved Semrush guide explicitly names AI Mode in its prompt-tracking description; that wording does not establish an Overview-reference function. For an Overview answer and its linked domain, seoClarity's dated description points to the AIO filter and the corresponding SERP Preview.
