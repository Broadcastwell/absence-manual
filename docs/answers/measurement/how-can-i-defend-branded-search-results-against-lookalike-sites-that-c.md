# How can I defend branded search results against lookalike sites that confuse LLMs?

The [Agent Gap method](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23197560) separates finding a company’s public endpoint from describing it correctly: one of seventy initial Describe tasks was labeled incorrect on 6 October 2026. For a suspected lookalike, preserve the disputed answer, its exact cited URL and your dated company reference before attributing the error or specifying a correction.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [published Agent Gap method and release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23197560) records the AppFolio Describe task as found and reached, but not correct. It is the single incorrect initial Describe task among seventy. This is a task classification, not a finding that a clone site caused the error.

**Reported.** Profound’s [FactCheck description](https://www.tryprofound.com/features/answer-engine-insights/factcheck), read on 29 October 2026, describes comparing factual claims with a company-controlled Knowledge Base and displaying source URLs. It distinguishes claim verification, source attribution and proposed remediation functions. Those are the supplier’s product statements, not a test we conducted.

**Reasoned.** A retained source URL and a contradictory official reference make a particular error reviewable. Neither the task label nor a supplier’s attribution feature establishes that a lookalike page caused a model’s answer.

## What this means for you

Read the claim itself. Profound’s FactCheck description distinguishes factual, actionable claims containing one verifiable assertion from subjective statements routed to Sentiment. For a suspected lookalike problem, that supplies a specific review object: the asserted company fact, rather than the tone of the whole answer.

The AppFolio task illustrates why access alone cannot settle accuracy. Its initial Describe session ended on 6 October with found and reached both true, while correct was false. A server request or a successful visit therefore would not resolve the disputed description recorded in that task.

For your company reference, Profound describes building its Knowledge Base by crawling a domain, uploading files or syncing Google Drive and Notion content. The saved product description says a claim view displays the ground truth and where it is defined. That is a concrete source artifact to inspect when distinguishing a company’s stated policy from a similar-looking name on another domain.
