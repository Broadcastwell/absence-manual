# What did the buyer report?

A buyer report is the buyer’s own account of how they found or assessed you. It differs from an assistant choosing a supplier: our [Head to Head study](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23202939) recorded 75 single-vendor selections among 359 scored answers from 22 September 2026. Those selections tell you what the engines returned, not what a buyer said, clicked or bought.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [Head to Head study method](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23202939) compares vendor pairs under retained buyer questions. Its 24 distinct category-and-question combinations contribute 359 scored answers; one further return is unscored. Seventy-five scored answers select one vendor. Counting that single-vendor outcome does not create seventy-five human decisions.

**Reported.** Our [AI Source Standard](https://docs.broadcastwell.com/ai-source-standard/#1-form-fields) defines a buyer’s shared source account separately from a referral observation or commercial event. The account needs the date and the discovery question the buyer chose to answer. A missing response remains a missing response.

**Reasoned.** The model-selection evidence is useful when reviewing what a buyer might have encountered, but it cannot establish that a particular buyer saw that answer. The study did not collect buyer testimony or purchases.

## What this means for you

In the retained construction question “Procore vs Buildertrend, which is better for a small GC”, the Head to Head table records fifteen scored answers: eleven select Buildertrend alone, three include both suppliers and one names neither. Those are classifications of engine returns. It does not supply the words of a general contractor who bought either tool.
