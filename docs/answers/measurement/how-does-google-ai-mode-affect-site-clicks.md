# How does Google AI Mode affect site clicks?

A citation total cannot tell you whether AI Mode raised or lowered site clicks. Our [22 September 2026 Index](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) recorded a HubSpot-domain URL in 3 of 30 Google AI Mode CRM answers. Those are three answer observations; estimating a traffic change requires the separate click records and their reporting scope.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [original CRM panel](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) contains ten questions with three Mode returns apiece. Three answers cite at least one HubSpot-owned host: one for a B2B sales team, one for replacing spreadsheets and one for a forecasting comparison. The forecasting answer contains both a HubSpot Academy URL and a HubSpot Knowledge Base URL, while contributing one answer to the domain count.

**Reported.** Google's [AI-features guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), read on 29 October 2026, places AI Mode and AI Overviews traffic within Search Console's Performance report under the Web search type. It separately discusses conversion and time-on-site analysis in analytics tools.

**Reasoned.** The three citing answers cannot be subtracted from, or added to, a Web click total: the observations have different units. This CRM capture contains no click or visitor records and was not a site-traffic experiment.

## What this means for you

Count the answer once. In the forecasting return, the recorded links point to an Academy reporting course and a Knowledge Base forecasting page. Two owned-host links in one response still contribute one to our three-answer figure. The retained URLs contain no event showing that a person opened either link.

The other Mode references lead to different HubSpot resources: a Sales product page in the sales-team answer and the CRM product page in the spreadsheet-replacement answer. That context belongs beside a citation chart. A change in which page is selected is an answer-source observation; it is different from a change in the number of people arriving at one landing page.

For your site's reporting, Google's Web grouping creates a specific boundary: it includes these AI features within overall search traffic. The cited guide does not turn that aggregate into a separate Mode-only click series.
