# What is a good AI visibility score?

There is no universal good AI visibility score in the published data. In answers captured on 22 September 2026, Dentrix was named in 120 of 149 dental practice answers, or 80.5 percent; AscendTMS appeared in 64 of 150 transportation answers, or 42.7 percent. Each had the largest naming count among the vendors checked in its own category, so you cannot carry one category's result over as the other's pass mark.

## What we measured

**Measured.** These records come from the Absence Index, release 2026-09, published 23 September 2026 under [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22907695](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695). Each category used ten fixed buyer questions, asked three times on ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. The dental collection retained 149 valid answers after one exclusion; transportation retained 150. Both were captured on 22 September 2026.

| Vendor and category | Named answers | Named rate | Wilson 95 percent interval |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Dentrix, dental practice management | 120 of 149 | 80.5 percent | 73.4 to 86.1 percent |
| AscendTMS, transportation management systems | 64 of 150 | 42.7 percent | 35.0 to 50.7 percent |

“Named” counts a vendor's appearance in an answer. It is separate from an own-domain citation, a recommendation in the buyer's context or a resulting enquiry. These dated counts and intervals describe the captured answers, not product quality, future presence or a universal benchmark.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** A target borrowed from Dentrix's 80.5 percent would put AscendTMS below the target even though AscendTMS had the largest naming count in the transportation collection. That cutoff imports a dental question set into a transportation decision. Read the category before judging the number.

- **If you sell software:** ask for the actual buyer questions before accepting either percentage as your target. A dental practice's evaluation and a freight broker's operating needs produce different shortlists.
- **If you buy software:** Dentrix's frequent appearance cannot tell you whether it meets your practice's requirements. Read the relevant answers and check the product facts behind the recommendation.
- **If you report results:** keep AscendTMS's 64 named answers beside its base of 150 and its transportation label. A slide that retains only 42.7 percent loses the context needed to interpret it.

For a later comparison, use the same measure and question set. The Manual's measurement chapters below explain the repeat-run and exclusion checks; a higher figure on a different collection does not establish an improvement.
