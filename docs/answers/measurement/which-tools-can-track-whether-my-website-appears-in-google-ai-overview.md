# Which tools can track whether my website appears in Google AI Overviews?

The [seoClarity guide](https://www.seoclarity.net/research/ai-overviews-impact) describes AIO query filters, linked-domain details and SERP Previews. Our [September Index](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) shows what a website-level count looks like: HubSpot's own site was a recorded reference in 6 of 30 CRM answer captures from Google AI Overviews on 22 September 2026.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [Index method and release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) use ten fixed CRM questions with three Overview returns apiece. All 30 belong to the original valid-answer base. Six contain a HubSpot-domain URL, counted once per answer even when an answer has several links. The same engine named HubSpot CRM in 20 of those 30 answers. This panel does not test seoClarity's accuracy, account access or a change in visitors.

**Reported.** seoClarity's page was last updated on 23 October 2025 and read on 29 October 2026. Its tracking section describes an AI Overviews filter in SERP Features, checks for whether a site or competitor is linked or referenced, and SERP Previews for reading the AIO content. The research discussed on that page uses UK desktop keyword data from September 2024 to September 2025.

**Reasoned.** HubSpot's six website-reference answers and twenty name-containing answers would give different rates from the same 30-return panel. A tool comparison therefore needs to identify which of those units its visibility field represents.

## What this means for you

Use the reference field. In the published seoClarity description, the AIO filter identifies triggering queries, the domain detail addresses the linked site, and SERP Preview shows the answer content. Those are different jobs within a website-appearance check. If your report concerns a page being referenced, a brand-name total alone leaves out the URL that connects the answer to your site.

Keep the referenced page with the count. Our six HubSpot-domain cases contain two distinct product URLs, rather than six different HubSpot pages. Four answer records reference the sales product URL and two reference the CRM product URL. That distinction lets you separate repeated appearances of a page from the number of pages an answer engine referenced. The recorded paths are `/products/sales` and `/products/crm`.
