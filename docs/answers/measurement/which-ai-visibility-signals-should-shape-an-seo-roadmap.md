# Which AI visibility signals should shape an SEO roadmap?

Let distinct signals lead to distinct investigations: missing names, missing source links, unsupported facts and unstable results ask different questions. In the Volume II collection captured from 18 to 23 July 2026, 9 of 85 companies had their own domains cited without being named in any of their ten answers. A single blended score would hide that source role when planning what to investigate next.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [Volume II method](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21586091) treated naming and own-domain citation as independent events. The convenience and challenger sample used ten questions per company, once per question, on one engine. The nine cited-but-never-named companies were a subset of the 85-company collection, not a current market estimate.

Facts need a separate review. [Wrong About You](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23270124) classified 2,922 statements about seventy vendors from answers captured on 22 and 23 September 2026: 1,924 true, 113 wrong, five stale and 880 unverifiable. Vendor pages were read on 8 October, and team review of flagged statements remains in progress.

**Reasoned.** These are different populations and methods, so their counts should remain separate. The first describes a company's observed role in answers; the second describes classified product statements. Neither proves which page change would raise visibility, generate revenue or improve product suitability.

## What this means for you

Assign each roadmap item an evidence question. A missing name calls for reading the buyer question and existing answers. A cited source with no named product calls for examining the role that source played. A disputed factual statement calls for a dated primary-source check.

Keep a short receipt beside the proposed work: observation, source, date, unresolved question and the change someone can inspect. This prevents a content task from inheriting an unmeasured promise about AI outcomes.

Review repeat variation before making a movement claim. A later answer can differ even when your page has not changed. Prioritisation is a reasoned business decision informed by these signals, not a ranking formula established by this research.
