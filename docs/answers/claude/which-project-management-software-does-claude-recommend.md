# Which project management software does Claude recommend?

ClickUp and Wrike tied at 18 of 30 Claude project-management answers captured on 22 September 2026, while Smartsheet appeared in 16. The tie leaves your project decision open. The underlying question records distinguish concurrent client work, spreadsheet replacement and distributed teams; the count measures naming rather than product quality.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Choose the problem your team needs to solve before breaking the tie. Coordinating client projects might lead you to inspect permissions and handoffs, while replacing spreadsheets might make the existing record structure the first thing to examine. Those are questions for a product evaluation, not capabilities established by the naming table.

Write one task that a prospective system must handle and ask for evidence of that task in the relevant edition. Retain the example record, the source document and any condition that would change the result. This gives your team something more useful to compare than two equal frequencies.

Read the question-level counts when an aggregate looks surprising. A system can accumulate names across several decisions and still be absent on the one that resembles your work. Conversely, a name appearing in one narrow question does not establish support for every project process in the panel.

If you maintain a project-software page, describe the supported task in concrete terms and attach the evidence for each capability. Keep an unanswered product question visible until its source is found. A zero row in this collection gives you an observation to investigate, without a measured explanation for the omission.
