# Which CRM software does Claude recommend?

Claude named both HubSpot CRM and Salesforce Sales Cloud in 24 of 30 CRM answers captured on 22 September 2026. Pipedrive appeared in 22 and Zoho CRM in 18. Yet none of the ten checked names appeared in the three answers about improving sales handoffs and follow-up. The collection therefore answers which names occurred, while leaving you to inspect how each answer treated the sales task.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [published September CRM record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) includes a 30-answer Claude panel, comprising three answers to each of ten buyer questions. The questions range from growing sales teams and complex pipelines to reporting, evaluation criteria and handoffs. Publication was on 23 September 2026, following captures on 22 September.

For HubSpot CRM, 24 of 30 is 80.0 percent; the descriptive Wilson 95 percent interval is 62.7 to 90.5 percent. It describes a name-matching result, with no inference here about the proportion of sales teams that would choose it.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Begin with the sales work you are trying to change. When a team replaces spreadsheets, the useful evaluation may concern who owns the next action and how a record moves between people. Write that requirement before importing a familiar product name from a broader CRM answer.

Keep a proposed tool, an asserted feature and evidence of the feature in separate fields. A buyer can then see which parts of an answer remain to be checked. HubSpot CRM's 24 appearances supply a historical naming result, without validating a reporting function or an integration under your account's conditions.

For a supplier comparison, read the source passages that support the particular sales claim. Do not infer a Claude-specific citation rate from the category's pooled source records. The engine-specific cells used here contain names; they are not a record of which domains Claude cited.
