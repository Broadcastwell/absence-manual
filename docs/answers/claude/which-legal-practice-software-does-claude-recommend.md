# Which legal practice software does Claude recommend?

Clio appeared in 25 of 30 Claude legal-practice answers captured on 22 September 2026; 8am MyCase appeared in 21 and Filevine in 19. For a litigation firm replacing an on-premise system, the useful next step is the matching question record, not a decision from those totals. The study recorded names rather than testing a practice's migration or professional requirements.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Write the migration problem before opening vendor pages. Which matter records need to move? Which billing records must remain usable? Use those requirements to decide what to inspect in a product demonstration. A repeated name tells you where an answer pointed, without proving that a migration would work for your firm.

Retain the exact product identity when you follow the record. A general vendor description can leave important conditions unresolved. For an export, document-management connection or permission setting, request the documentation for the edition you would actually use and record the source date beside your requirement.

Keep the smaller-practice question separate from the litigation-migration question in your notes. Moving a candidate between them without checking its support loses the distinction shown by the 8am MyCase counts. You can reasonably retain the product for investigation while marking a particular requirement unknown.

If you publish a legal-software comparison, tie each factual description to its supporting source. Do not describe an observed zero as a failing in the product, and do not turn this naming measure into a claim that confidentiality, professional suitability or migration outcomes were assessed.
