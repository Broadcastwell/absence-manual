# Which radiology software does Perplexity recommend?

In the radiology panel captured on 22 September 2026, Perplexity named Sectra in 18 of 30 answers, Intelerad in 15 and Visage Imaging in 14. Eighteen of the 24 checked vendors appeared at least once. These counts locate names in recorded software answers; they do not establish clinical performance, interoperability or a purchasing recommendation.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [published release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) combines three Perplexity runs of ten fixed imaging-software questions, giving thirty valid answers. Sectra's 18 of 30 produces 60.0%, with a descriptive 95% Wilson interval of 42.3% to 75.4%. All captures in this engine/category panel are dated 22 September 2026, with publication on 23 September.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Start with the system and setting in the question. An outpatient RIS request does not ask the same thing as a hospital PACS comparison. Keep that distinction in the notes you take from a saved answer, rather than merging every name into one undifferentiated shortlist.

For a proposed integration, retrieve the relevant product and interface documentation. Identify the edition and operation the answer describes, and record any point the available source cannot establish. A familiar name cannot resolve that gap.

If you publish a comparison, label it as Perplexity naming in this dated collection. The six zero rows and the exact question set belong beside the nonzero counts. The observations do not identify why an engine selected a name or support claims about patient outcomes.
