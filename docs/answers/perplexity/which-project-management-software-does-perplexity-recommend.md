# Which project management software does Perplexity recommend?

The saved Perplexity project-management answers name Asana in 19 of 30, ClickUp in 18 and Wrike in 14, with captures dated 22 September 2026. Nine of the ten checked platforms appear somewhere in the panel. This is a naming result across several coordination problems, not a test of which platform will deliver your projects.

## What we measured

**Measured.** Each of the ten project-management questions has three valid Perplexity runs in the [September Index data](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695). Asana's 19 of 30 corresponds to 63.3%, with a descriptive 95% Wilson interval of 45.5% to 78.1%. The answers were captured on 22 September 2026 and published in the release the next day.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Describe the coordination difficulty before choosing which saved records to read. Concurrent client projects, distributed work and planning across functions all occur in this bank. Keep the corresponding question with each count you use in an internal recommendation.

Turn a suggested platform into a short list of claims to verify. For a services firm with poor visibility, that might mean examining the exact reporting or planning operation described by the answer. The published counts do not show whether your information can be assembled that way.

Retain Notion's zero row and the two name-free question groups in any summary. Neither should disappear because they interrupt a tidy list of products. If your later collection uses different project questions, show that change before comparing the resulting rates.
