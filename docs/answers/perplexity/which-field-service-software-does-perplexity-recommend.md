# Which field service software does Perplexity recommend?

A one-person HVAC question and a commercial-contractor question produced different Perplexity lists. Across the thirty field-service answers captured on 22 September 2026, Housecall Pro appeared in 21, ServiceTitan in 16 and FieldPulse in 15. Thirteen of the 22 checked vendors were named somewhere, without a tested recommendation for your trade or crew.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The fixed field-service bank covers ten questions, from a one-person HVAC business to commercial mechanical and fire-protection contractors. Three Perplexity runs per question produced thirty valid answers. In the [23 September release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695), Housecall Pro's 21 of 30 gives 70.0%, with a descriptive 95% Wilson interval of 52.1% to 83.3%, based on captures from the preceding day.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Locate the question nearest your business before making a vendor list. A one-person HVAC operator's scheduling task and a commercial contractor's service requirements need their own evidence. The contrast between those saved answers is more informative than treating 21 of 30 as a universal choice.

Take one claim from the answer and check the exact product documentation. Specify the task, trade and edition you are evaluating. If an answer mentions fire-protection work, retain the source that supports that particular statement rather than substituting a general category description.

Keep all nine zero rows when you share the Perplexity results. BigChange or ServiceMax being absent here does not establish why they were omitted. A proposed explanation needs an answer passage or another source; the count alone provides neither.
