# Which transportation software does Perplexity recommend?

The ten-truck carrier question returned AscendTMS in all three Perplexity answers captured on 22 September 2026. Across the thirty transportation answers, AscendTMS appeared in 15 and Alvys in ten; nineteen of the 25 checked vendors were named somewhere. Those are the observed names in this study, with a carrier, broker or shipper's purchasing choice still to establish.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The transportation panel contains thirty valid Perplexity answers from ten predefined questions and three runs per question. The [release receipt](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) was published on 23 September 2026, following the 22 September captures. AscendTMS's observed 15 of 30 is 50.0%, with a descriptive 95% Wilson interval of 33.2% to 66.8%.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Choose the operating role before interpreting the list. The small-carrier question and the brokerage question produced different observed names. Save that wording beside any table you take to an operations or purchasing discussion.

Check an answer's specific claim against documentation for the relevant product. A broad vendor identity may not establish the module or connection that your freight workflow needs. Keep the source passage and any unresolved detail in the comparison rather than filling the gap from familiarity with the name.

When reporting omissions, retain all six zero rows and describe the checked set. Truckbase occurs in one fixed comparison question but is not one of this table's checked vendor identities. Its absence from the table is a scope boundary, not a zero measured under the same matching rules.
