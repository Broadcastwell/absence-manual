# Which legal practice software does Perplexity recommend?

On 22 September 2026, Clio appeared in 24 of thirty captured Perplexity legal-practice answers. The same panel named 8am MyCase in 20 answers and PracticePanther in 19. That records exposure within these questions, with no product test or endorsement of a law firm's choice.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [September release and receipt](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695) retain ten legal-software questions repeated three times on Perplexity. There were no exclusions from this thirty-answer panel. Clio's observed rate is 80.0%, with a descriptive 95% Wilson interval of 62.7% to 90.5%. The capture date is 22 September 2026; publication is dated 23 September.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Separate discovering a possible supplier from checking how your firm would use it. A one-attorney practice and a litigation firm migrating existing records have different questions in this bank. Read the one that describes your decision, including the conditions surrounding each named product.

For any claim about a firm's required operation, ask for the applicable product source and edition. Retain the exact passage alongside the AI answer. A high naming count provides no substitute for reviewing the controls or terms your firm needs.

When reporting these results, preserve the difference between unaided names and comparisons that already contain a brand. A Clio-versus-MyCase prompt introduces both identities before the answer begins. Its observations belong in the panel, but should remain identifiable when someone asks how a shortlist was formed.
