# Which CRM software does Perplexity recommend?

HubSpot CRM, Pipedrive and Salesforce Sales Cloud each appeared in 22 of 30 Perplexity CRM answers captured on 22 September 2026. Eight of the ten checked vendors appeared somewhere in that panel. These are name matches in saved answers, rather than tested recommendations for a sales team.

## What we measured

**Measured.** The [September Index release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695), published on 23 September 2026, records three Perplexity runs for each of ten fixed CRM questions. All thirty answers are valid. HubSpot CRM's 22 of 30 is 73.3%, with a descriptive 95% Wilson interval of 55.6% to 85.8%; Pipedrive and Salesforce Sales Cloud have the same count.

The totals hide a useful difference. For the question about replacing spreadsheets, HubSpot CRM, Pipedrive, Salesforce Sales Cloud and Microsoft Dynamics 365 each appeared in all three answers. The buyer-evaluation checklist named none of the checked vendors in its three answers. That checklist is still part of the base.

The matching rule counts a vendor once within an answer. It does not grade the surrounding claim or record whether a cited page supports it. Several names can share an answer, and the alphabetical rows are not a preference order.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** If you are replacing spreadsheets, start with that question's records before opening the broader totals. Write down the handoff, forecast or reporting task your team needs to perform. A familiar CRM name gives you something to investigate; it does not supply the missing workflow requirements.

Check the actual product and edition behind a claim. Microsoft Dynamics 365's category identity, for example, is less specific than a documented operation in a particular application. Retain the source passage beside the answer when you evaluate that operation.

For a visibility comparison, keep the thirty-answer panel together. Copper and Nimble's zero rows belong in the report alongside the three 22-answer totals. Removing an answer because it offers a checklist rather than product names would change what the rate means.
