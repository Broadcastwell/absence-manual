# Which property management software does Perplexity recommend?

Buildium was absent from the small-landlord question's three Perplexity answers, yet appeared in 23 of 30 answers across the residential-management category. AppFolio appeared in 19 and DoorLoop in 17, in captures dated 22 September 2026. Seventeen of the 22 checked vendors were named somewhere; the count does not settle a particular portfolio's choice.

## What we measured

**Measured.** Ten fixed residential-property questions supplied three Perplexity answers each. All thirty runs are valid in the [September release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22907695). ## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** State the portfolio and task before reading a category total. A landlord with a few properties may need different evidence from a growing management company. Use the corresponding question cells to see how that context accompanied the names in this collection.

Check the particular product statement that attracts your attention. If an answer says a system handles a workflow, find the dated product source and retain the relevant terms or edition boundary. A high naming count cannot establish a contract term or the way your own accounts would reconcile.

For a vendor visibility report, include Avail, Condo Control, ManageCasa, Property Matrix and Rentvine's zero observations. Keep their identities as published and the thirty-answer base visible. The study does not supply a reason for those absences or a promise that a content change will reverse them.
