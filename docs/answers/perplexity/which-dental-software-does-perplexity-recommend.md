# Which dental software does Perplexity recommend?

Fifteen of the 20 checked dental vendors appeared in Perplexity's thirty answers captured on 22 September 2026. Open Dental was named in 23 of 30, while Curve Dental and Dentrix each appeared in 21. The office context changes the observed list; suitability for your practice still needs its own evidence.

## What this means for you

**Reasoned.** Begin with the practice you actually run. A new office, a very small office and a multi-location organisation ask different software questions in this collection. Use the relevant saved answers to build a list for further checking rather than treating the category total as a procurement decision.

Read the product documentation behind each stated scheduling, billing or data-transfer claim. Keep the product edition visible in your notes. A name count cannot verify that your existing records will move cleanly or that a quoted arrangement applies to your practice.

If you represent a dental vendor, compare the exact matched identity with the public record before describing an absence. Five checked vendors have zero names in this Perplexity panel. Their zeros record this study's question set, not a finding about clinical performance or continued availability.
