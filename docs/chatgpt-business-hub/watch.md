# Is the ChatGPT Business Hub available yet?

No. The ChatGPT Business Hub was reported on 6 November 2026, and at our last check, on 8 November 2026, it was still not released and OpenAI had no launch page for it. This page lists where we look for availability and records the result of each check. We give no launch date and make no forecast.

Updated 9 November 2026.

## Where we check

| Place | What would show availability |
| --- | --- |
| OpenAI's help center | An article explaining how a business claims or verifies itself in ChatGPT |
| OpenAI's announcements | A post or release note introducing the tool by name |
| The ChatGPT interface | The "Claim your business in ChatGPT" banner opening a working claim flow |
| The two report sources | An update from Juozas Kaziukėnas on LinkedIn or Barry Schwartz on Search Engine Roundtable saying the tool has opened |

A screenshot in a report is evidence that the tool exists in some accounts. It is not a release, so we record it as reported until OpenAI describes the tool itself or the claim flow works.

## Check log

| Date checked | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 8 November 2026 | Reported on 6 November 2026, not released. No OpenAI launch page and no published verification method. |

## What happens on release

Clients in Care and Program get the launch-day claim, part 5 of the [readiness work](index.md#readiness-offer): we claim the business on the day OpenAI opens the tool to it, then start the monthly [reconciliation report](reconciliation-report.md). Anyone can prepare before then with the [readiness checklist](index.md#ownership-verification), a [business facts file](business-facts-file.md) and a [context pack](context-pack.md).
