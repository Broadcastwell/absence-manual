# How do you cite Broadcastwell research and data?

To cite Broadcastwell research and data, cite the version DOI of the exact release you used, with its version and date. For the Absence Index September 2026 release that is DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22907695; for the State of GEO Volume III it is DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21789120. Ready-made APA, Chicago, BibTeX and RIS entries for both, and for every other DOI and SSRN id we publish, are below.

Updated 9 November 2026.

Download every entry at once: [BibTeX file](assets/citations/broadcastwell-research.bib){: download="broadcastwell-research.bib" } and [RIS file](assets/citations/broadcastwell-research.ris){: download="broadcastwell-research.ris" }. Both hold the same 21 records.

## The Absence Index, release 2026-09

The September 2026 release covers 14 B2B software categories and 286 checked vendors, answered by ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode on 22 and 23 September 2026. Cite this version DOI for any figure from it. The concept DOI, [10.5281/zenodo.22150321](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22150321), names the whole series of Index releases and is the right choice only when you mean the series.

**APA (7th edition)**

Broadcastwell. (2026). *The Absence Index: September 2026 Release* (Version 2026-09) [Data set]. Zenodo. https://doi.org/<wbr>10.5281/zenodo.22907695

**Chicago (author-date)**

Broadcastwell. 2026. "The Absence Index: September 2026 Release." Version 2026-09. Zenodo, September 23, 2026. https://doi.org/<wbr>10.5281/zenodo.22907695

## Which details to follow

Titles, versions, dates and authors follow each DOI record as we read it on 9 November 2026. For the three State of GEO volumes we add the volume name that our [data page](https://broadcastwell.com/data) uses, because the records share one title. A few of our pages show a shorter title for the same work; where they differ, cite the title in the DOI record. The registration titles follow our [research pages](research/index.md), and a registration is a plan, not a result.

A release is never edited in place. A correction gets a new version and a new DOI, so a citation to a version DOI always points to the files you read. Our [article archive](articles.md) lists every study by date, and our [model release tracker](model-releases.md) records provider changes between captures.
