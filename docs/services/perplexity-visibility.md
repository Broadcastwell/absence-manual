# What do we do about Perplexity visibility?

For Perplexity visibility we do what every offer does for each engine, with Perplexity reported apart: put your agreed buyer questions to Perplexity, keep each answer and every source it cites, repair the page or outside source behind a wrong or missing answer, and re-check. No offer covers Perplexity alone, and we cannot make Perplexity include you.

Updated 9 November 2026.

Our captures ask ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode the same questions. Perplexity stood out in the September 2026 Index by the sheer number of sources it cited, and by how few of them were vendor websites.

## Perplexity in the September Index

| Measure, September 2026 | Perplexity | All five engines |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Valid target answers | 420 of 420 attempts | 2,095 of 2,100 attempts |
| Cited entries | 8,298 | 18,893 |
| Answers with no cited entry | 0 of 420, 0.0% (0.0% to 0.9%) | 39 of 2,095, 1.9% (1.4% to 2.5%) |
| Vendor sites in cited entries | 1,599 of 8,298, 19.3% (18.4% to 20.1%) | 5,766 of 18,893, 30.5% (29.9% to 31.2%) |
| Review platforms in cited entries | 979 of 8,298, 11.8% (11.1% to 12.5%) | 1,706 of 18,893, 9.0% (8.6% to 9.4%) |
| Checked-vendor names | 1,642 | 8,085 |
| Vendor-question cells named in one or two of three runs | 200 of 2,860, 7.0% (6.1% to 8.0%) | 1,259 of 14,192, 8.9% (8.4% to 9.4%) |

**Measured.** Perplexity cited 8,298 entries across 420 valid answers, more than ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode together. Vendor sites were the smallest share of any engine, 19.3%, while review platforms (11.8%), directories and listicles (7.9%) and news and trade media (6.2%) each took a larger share than on ChatGPT. Half of its entries, 50.4%, sat in "other". Its answers carried 1,642 checked-vendor names in all.

## Perplexity changes we track

The Perplexity changelog, read 9 November 2026, names models it added in entries dated 27 July to 5 October 2026, including one dated 21 September 2026, the day before our capture. Perplexity offers models from other providers inside its product, and the changelog does not say which model answers a signed-out search, so we mark the date and infer nothing. The [model release tracker](../model-releases.md) keeps the full list.

## Where Perplexity work starts

**Reasoned.** Since vendor sites were under a fifth of Perplexity's citations, a page on your own site is rarely the whole repair. Care and Program work the outside pages Perplexity cites for your category, each change reported with its receipt, and Answer Repair can target an outside source when the evidence points there. That is a reading of this record, not a result.
