# What does Google AI Mode visibility work involve?

Google AI Mode visibility work means asking AI Mode your agreed buyer questions on their own line, apart from Google AI Overviews, keeping each answer and its sources, repairing the page or source behind a missing or wrong answer, and asking again on the same terms. It is part of every offer, never sold alone, and nothing we do can make AI Mode name a vendor.

Updated 9 November 2026.

We record ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode, and the two Google products separately because they answer differently. AI Mode had the least stable answers of the five in our September 2026 Index.

| Checked-vendor names | 1,556 | 8,085 |
| Vendor-question cells named in one or two of three runs | 345 of 2,860, 12.1% (10.9% to 13.3%) | 1,259 of 14,192, 8.9% (8.4% to 9.4%) |

**Measured.** In 345 of 2,860 vendor-question cells with three valid runs, Google AI Mode named the vendor in only one or two of them, 12.1%, the highest share of the five engines. It also had the most valid answers without any cited entry, 29 of 420 (6.9%), and the fewest cited entries overall, 1,577. Vendor sites took 39.1% of those entries, the second highest share after ChatGPT. AI Mode produced 1,556 checked-vendor names.

## Google changes we track for AI Mode

Google posts dated 2 September and 13 October 2026 describe new models reaching AI Mode in Search, with Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers named in the stated scope. Under our [release annotation rules](../model-releases.md), a paid-plan scope tells us nothing about what our captures used, so we record the scope and infer nothing further. Google Analytics' default channel group, read 9 November 2026, counts visits from AI Overviews and AI Mode as Organic Search and excludes both from its AI Assistant channel, so AI Mode visits do not appear there.

## Where Google AI Mode work starts

**Reasoned.** With one cell in eight changing across three runs, a single AI Mode answer is weak evidence in either direction. We judge an AI Mode absence across the repeats, and treat a change between captures as real only when the 95 percent intervals separate, as our [published method](https://broadcastwell.com/methodology) requires.
