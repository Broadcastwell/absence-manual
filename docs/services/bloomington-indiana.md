## Asking a question that names Bloomington

A buyer question can name the city. For example, a property management software company could choose "Which property management software do landlords in Bloomington, Indiana use?" as its live question. That is an example of wording, not a measured result.

Two scope points apply to such a question. First, the engines read the city in the question text; our capture uses one chosen country, United States by default, and our method reports declared location and network country separately rather than standing in for a searcher on a Bloomington street.
