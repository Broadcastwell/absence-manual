# How do we work on Google AI Overviews visibility?

We work on Google AI Overviews visibility as one of five engines in every offer, recorded separately from Google AI Mode: we search your agreed buyer questions, keep the Overview and every link it shows, repair the page or source behind a wrong or missing answer, and search again. When no Overview appears, we record that and leave it out of both count and base. We cannot make Google show or change one.

Updated 9 November 2026.

The five engines are ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. Google AI Overviews is the one engine in our captures whose answer can be absent: a search may return no Overview at all.

**Measured.** Of 420 target attempts, 5 were excluded, all on Google AI Overviews: 4 returned no AI Overview for the query and one returned Google's message "We couldn't get valid results for this search." The 415 valid Overviews cited 3,050 entries. Video pages took 9.1% and forums 8.9%, the largest shares of either class among the five engines; vendor sites took 29.1%. Google AI Overviews produced the fewest checked-vendor names, 1,352.

## Google changes we track

Google's page "AI features and your website", last updated 10 December 2025 and read 9 November 2026, says traffic from sites shown in AI Overviews and AI Mode is part of overall search traffic in Search Console, reported in the Performance report under the Web search type. Google's post "More ways to connect with the web from Search", dated 15 October 2026, describes clearer inline links and more source context in AI Mode and AI Overviews; it came after our capture, and our [model release tracker](../model-releases.md) records it as a product change.

## Where Google AI Overviews work starts

**Reasoned.** Overviews leaned on video and forum pages more than any other engine in this record, so for a vendor left out of an Overview we read the cited videos and threads as closely as your own pages. Because an Overview may not appear, we first confirm one exists for the question before treating an absence as yours.
