# How do we work on AI answers for construction software?

Our work for construction software uses one dated record as its baseline: across 150 valid construction answers captured on 22 September 2026, Procore appeared in 109 and Buildertrend in 83, out of 22 checked vendors. Each offer measures your agreed construction questions, fixes a page or source we can show sits behind an answer, and re-checks the same questions.

Updated 9 November 2026.

The engines are ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode, reported one by one. No offer measures one engine alone, and nothing we do makes an engine name a construction vendor.

## The construction record

| Construction record, September 2026 | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Checked construction vendors | 22 |
| Valid construction answers | 150 of 150 planned |
| Excluded construction attempts | none |
| Procore named | 109 of 150, 72.7% (65.0% to 79.2%); own domain cited in 30 |
| Buildertrend named | 83 of 150, 55.3% (47.3% to 63.1%); own domain cited in 34 |
| Contractor Foreman named | 77 of 150, 51.3% (43.4% to 59.2%); own domain cited in 31 |
| Top three share of all construction vendor names | 269 of 555, 48.5% (44.3% to 52.6%) |
| Construction vendors never named | 4 of 22 |
| Construction answers with no cited entry | 2 of 150, 1.3% (0.4% to 4.7%) |

Construction answers carried 1,389 cited entries: vendor sites took 46.9% (44.3% to 49.6%), review platforms 8.8% and forums 3.2%, while 32.3% fell in our "other" class of hosts with no single role.

## Where construction work starts

**Reasoned.** Vendor sites held 46.9% of construction citations, so work for a construction vendor usually begins on its own product, pricing and comparison pages. Review platforms held 8.8% and directories 3.0%; we read the exact construction listing an answer cited before proposing a change. None of this is a measured effect of a change.
