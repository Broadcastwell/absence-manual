# How do our offers apply to dental software in AI answers?

Dental software vendors start from a measured record, not a promise. The September 2026 Index checked 20 dental vendors in 149 valid answers captured on 22 September 2026; Dentrix was named in 120 and Open Dental in 118. Our offers capture your own dental questions, repair what the evidence supports, and measure again.

Updated 9 November 2026.

Every dental capture covers ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode, each kept apart in the report. None of our offers buys a placement, and none covers a single engine on its own.

## The dental record

| Dental record, September 2026 | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Checked dental vendors | 20 |
| Valid dental answers | 149 of 150 planned |
| Excluded dental attempts | 1, all Google AI Overviews: "no AI Overview returned for this query" |
| Dentrix named | 120 of 149, 80.5% (73.4% to 86.1%); own domain cited in 13 |
| Open Dental named | 118 of 149, 79.2% (72.0% to 84.9%); own domain cited in 21 |
| Curve Dental named | 115 of 149, 77.2% (69.8% to 83.2%); own domain cited in 37 |

Dental answers carried 1,241 cited entries: vendor sites took 28.0% (25.5% to 30.5%), review platforms 10.4% and directories and listicles 6.4%, while 47.5% fell in our "other" class of hosts with no single role.
