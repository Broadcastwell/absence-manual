# How do we read AI answers for transportation software?

Transportation software vendors start from a measured record, not a promise. The September 2026 Index checked 25 transportation vendors in 150 valid answers captured on 22 September 2026; AscendTMS was named in 64 and Alvys in 58. Our offers capture your own transportation questions, repair what the evidence supports, and measure again.

Updated 9 November 2026.

The engines are ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode, reported one by one. No offer measures one engine alone, and nothing we do makes an engine name a transportation vendor.

## The transportation record

| Transportation record, September 2026 | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Checked transportation vendors | 25 |
| Valid transportation answers | 150 of 150 planned |
| Excluded transportation attempts | none |
| AscendTMS named | 64 of 150, 42.7% (35.0% to 50.7%); own domain cited in 9 |
| Alvys named | 58 of 150, 38.7% (31.2% to 46.7%); own domain cited in 29 |
| Oracle Transportation Management named | 43 of 150, 28.7% (22.0% to 36.4%); own domain cited in 9 |

Of 1,430 cited transportation entries, 22.2% went to vendor sites (20.2% to 24.5%), 7.9% to review platforms and 7.1% to news and trade media; 51.5% went to the "other" class for hosts with mixed roles.

Our [page on transportation software missing from AI answers](../../answers/industries/why-is-my-transportation-software-missing-from-ai-answers.md) reads the low and zero counts, [the transportation cited-host page](../../answers/industries/which-sources-do-ai-answers-cite-for-transportation-software.md) lists domains, and the Index keeps the [AscendTMS record](https://index.broadcastwell.com/vendors/ascendtms/transportation-management-systems/).

## Where transportation work starts

**Reasoned.** Vendor sites held only 22.2% of transportation citations, so outside pages carry much of the transportation record. Review platforms held 7.9% and directories 5.7%; we read the exact transportation listing an answer cited before proposing a change. With 51.5% of transportation entries in "other", we open each cited page before choosing one to work on. None of this is a measured effect of a change.
