# How do we approach AI answers about radiology software?

Our work for radiology software uses one dated record as its baseline: across 150 valid radiology answers captured on 22 September 2026, Sectra appeared in 87 and Intelerad in 80, out of 24 checked vendors. Each offer measures your agreed radiology questions, fixes a page or source we can show sits behind an answer, and re-checks the same questions.

Updated 9 November 2026.

Your radiology results come engine by engine from ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. An engine decides its own answer; our part is the evidence and the page or source repair.

## The radiology record

| Radiology record, September 2026 | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Checked radiology vendors | 24 |
| Valid radiology answers | 150 of 150 planned |
| Excluded radiology attempts | none |
| Sectra named | 87 of 150, 58.0% (50.0% to 65.6%); own domain cited in 19 |
| Intelerad named | 80 of 150, 53.3% (45.4% to 61.1%); own domain cited in 31 |
| Visage Imaging named | 65 of 150, 43.3% (35.7% to 51.3%); own domain cited in 18 |

Radiology answers carried 1,297 cited entries: vendor sites took 22.1% (19.9% to 24.4%), review platforms 6.9% and forums 6.8%, while 54.0% fell in our "other" class of hosts with no single role.

## Where radiology work starts

**Reasoned.** Only 22.1% of radiology citations went to vendor sites; most radiology evidence sits elsewhere. Review platforms held 6.9% and directories 1.7%; we read the exact radiology listing an answer cited before proposing a change. With 54.0% of radiology entries in "other", we open each cited page before choosing one to work on. None of this is a measured effect of a change.
