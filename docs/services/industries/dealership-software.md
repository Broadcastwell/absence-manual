# Where do we start with dealership software in AI answers?

In dealership software, CDK Global and DealerCenter were the names the engines gave most often in September 2026: 90 and 79 of 150 valid answers captured on 22 September 2026, among 22 checked dealership vendors. That record is where our offers begin; the work then measures, repairs and re-checks your own dealership questions.

Updated 9 November 2026.

We record ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode separately for every dealership question. We cannot make any of them name you; we can show what each said and fix what is wrong at its source.

## The dealership record

| Dealership record, September 2026 | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Checked dealership vendors | 22 |
| Valid dealership answers | 150 of 150 planned |
| Excluded dealership attempts | none |
| CDK Global named | 90 of 150, 60.0% (52.0% to 67.5%); own domain cited in 13 |
| DealerCenter named | 79 of 150, 52.7% (44.7% to 60.5%); own domain cited in 23 |
| Tekion named | 76 of 150, 50.7% (42.7% to 58.6%); own domain cited in 20 |

Dealership answers carried 1,307 cited entries: vendor sites took 21.7% (19.6% to 24.0%), review platforms 10.6% and directories and listicles 4.8%, while 55.0% fell in our "other" class of hosts with no single role.

## Where dealership work starts

**Reasoned.** Only 21.7% of dealership citations went to vendor sites; most dealership evidence sits elsewhere. Review platforms held 10.6%, above the 9.5% median across the fourteen categories, so current dealership listings get checked early. With 55.0% of dealership entries in "other", we open each cited page before choosing one to work on. None of this is a measured effect of a change.
