# What can AI answer work do for loan origination software?

What our offers do for loan origination software follows from the published loan origination record: 25 checked vendors and 150 valid answers captured on 22 September 2026, with nCino named in 92 and MeridianLink in 81. We put your loan origination questions to each engine, fix the page or source behind a wrong answer, and ask the same questions again.

Updated 9 November 2026.

Every loan origination capture covers ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode, each kept apart in the report. None of our offers buys a placement, and none covers a single engine on its own.

The 1,399 entries cited in loan origination answers split as 27.2% vendor sites (24.9% to 29.6%), 11.6% review platforms and 6.9% news and trade media. 45.4% sat in "other", a class for hosts without one clear role.
