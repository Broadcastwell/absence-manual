# How do we handle project management software in AI answers?

What our offers do for project management software follows from the published project management record: 10 checked vendors and 150 valid answers captured on 22 and 23 September 2026, with Asana named in 97 and ClickUp in 80. We put your project management questions to each engine, fix the page or source behind a wrong answer, and ask the same questions again.

Updated 9 November 2026.

Every project management capture covers ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode, each kept apart in the report. None of our offers buys a placement, and none covers a single engine on its own.

## Where project management work starts

**Reasoned.** Vendor sites held only 23.6% of project management citations, so outside pages carry much of the project management record. Review platforms held 1.2%, a minor project management source in this record. With 56.8% of project management entries in "other", we open each cited page before choosing one to work on. None of this is a measured effect of a change.
