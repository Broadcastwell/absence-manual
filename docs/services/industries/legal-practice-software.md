# How does AI answer work apply to legal practice software?

What our offers do for legal practice software follows from the published legal practice record: 21 checked vendors and 150 valid answers captured on 22 September 2026, with Clio named in 126 and 8am MyCase in 96. We put your legal practice questions to each engine, fix the page or source behind a wrong answer, and ask the same questions again.

Updated 9 November 2026.

Every legal practice capture covers ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode, each kept apart in the report. None of our offers buys a placement, and none covers a single engine on its own.
