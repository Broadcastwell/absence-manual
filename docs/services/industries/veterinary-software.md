# How do our offers handle veterinary software?

What our offers do for veterinary software follows from the published veterinary record: 19 checked vendors and 149 valid answers captured on 22 September 2026, with ezyVet named in 115 and IDEXX (Cornerstone, Neo) in 90. We put your veterinary questions to each engine, fix the page or source behind a wrong answer, and ask the same questions again.

Updated 9 November 2026.

We record ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode separately for every veterinary question. We cannot make any of them name you; we can show what each said and fix what is wrong at its source.

Veterinary answers carried 1,366 cited entries: vendor sites took 30.8% (28.4% to 33.3%), review platforms 17.7% and directories and listicles 5.8%, while 42.5% fell in our "other" class of hosts with no single role.

Our [page on veterinary software missing from AI answers](../../answers/industries/why-is-my-veterinary-software-missing-from-ai-answers.md) reads the low and zero counts, [the veterinary cited-host page](../../answers/industries/which-sources-do-ai-answers-cite-for-veterinary-software.md) lists domains, and the Index keeps the [ezyVet record](https://index.broadcastwell.com/vendors/ezyvet/veterinary-practice-management/).
