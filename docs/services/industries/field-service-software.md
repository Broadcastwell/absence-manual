# What does AI answer work cover for field service software?

What our offers do for field service software follows from the published field service record: 22 checked vendors and 150 valid answers captured on 22 September 2026, with Housecall Pro named in 108 and ServiceTitan in 102. We put your field service questions to each engine, fix the page or source behind a wrong answer, and ask the same questions again.

Updated 9 November 2026.

The engines are ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode, reported one by one. No offer measures one engine alone, and nothing we do makes an engine name a field service vendor.

## The field service record

| Field service record, September 2026 | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Checked field service vendors | 22 |
| Valid field service answers | 150 of 150 planned |
| Excluded field service attempts | none |
| Housecall Pro named | 108 of 150, 72.0% (64.3% to 78.6%); own domain cited in 43 |
| ServiceTitan named | 102 of 150, 68.0% (60.2% to 74.9%); own domain cited in 35 |
| Workiz named | 58 of 150, 38.7% (31.2% to 46.7%); own domain cited in 20 |
| Top three share of all field service vendor names | 268 of 578, 46.4% (42.3% to 50.4%) |
| Field service vendors never named | 7 of 22 |
| Field service answers with no cited entry | 9 of 150, 6.0% (3.2% to 11.0%) |

The 1,264 entries cited in field service answers split as 43.9% vendor sites (41.2% to 46.7%), 9.0% directories and listicles and 6.7% review platforms. 29.6% sat in "other", a class for hosts without one clear role.

## Where field service work starts

**Reasoned.** With 43.9% of field service citations on vendor sites, your own field service pages come first. Review platforms held 6.7% and directories 9.0%; we read the exact field service listing an answer cited before proposing a change. None of this is a measured effect of a change.
