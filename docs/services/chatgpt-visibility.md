# What does our ChatGPT visibility work include?

Our ChatGPT visibility work is the five-engine work every offer runs, with ChatGPT read on its own line: we record what ChatGPT answers to your buyer questions, find the page or source behind a missing or wrong answer, repair it with facts you approve, and ask again. No offer measures ChatGPT alone, and nothing we do can make ChatGPT name you.

Updated 11 November 2026.

Every capture covers ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode with identical wording, so a ChatGPT result always sits beside four others. The figures below come from the September 2026 Absence Index: 140 fixed buyer questions across fourteen software categories, three scheduled runs each.

## What ChatGPT did in the September Index

| Measure, September 2026 | ChatGPT | All five engines |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Valid target answers | 420 of 420 attempts | 2,095 of 2,100 attempts |
| Cited entries | 1,993 | 18,893 |
| Answers with no cited entry | 0 of 420, 0.0% (0.0% to 0.9%) | 39 of 2,095, 1.9% (1.4% to 2.5%) |
| Vendor sites in cited entries | 1,708 of 1,993, 85.7% (84.1% to 87.2%) | 5,766 of 18,893, 30.5% (29.9% to 31.2%) |
| Review platforms in cited entries | 5 of 1,993, 0.3% (0.1% to 0.6%) | 1,706 of 18,893, 9.0% (8.6% to 9.4%) |
| Checked-vendor names | 1,496 | 8,085 |
| Vendor-question cells named in one or two of three runs | 159 of 2,860, 5.6% (4.8% to 6.5%) | 1,259 of 14,192, 8.9% (8.4% to 9.4%) |

**Measured.** ChatGPT leaned on vendor sites more than any other engine: 85.7% of its 1,993 cited entries went to the websites of software vendors, against 30.5% for the five engines together. Review platforms supplied only 5 entries. Government and standards bodies, a rare class elsewhere, supplied 43 (2.2%), and 11.2% fell in our "other" class. Every one of the 420 valid ChatGPT answers carried at least one cited entry, and ChatGPT had the lowest share of vendor-question cells that changed between its three runs.

## OpenAI changes we track for ChatGPT

OpenAI's ChatGPT release notes, read 9 November 2026, dated six model entries between 6 August and 26 October 2026. The entry dated 22 September 2026, our first capture day, was scoped to ChatGPT Work and Codex, which OpenAI describes as separate from the models in Chat. The 6 August entry concerned the chat experience before our capture, and the next Chat tab change is dated 7 October 2026, after it. Our [model release tracker](../model-releases.md) lists each one with its scope, and we never credit a change in a ChatGPT count to a release.

## What this means for a ChatGPT repair

**Reasoned.** Because 85.7% of ChatGPT's cited entries were vendor sites, start with your own site when ChatGPT leaves you out or gets a fact wrong: the product, pricing and comparison facts a buyer question needs, stated plainly on pages a reader can open. A three-run ChatGPT miss was also the steadiest pattern in this record, so a repeated absence is worth investigating rather than waiting out. Neither point predicts the next ChatGPT answer.
