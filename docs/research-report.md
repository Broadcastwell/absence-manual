# Can you download a research report without a form?

Yes. Every Broadcastwell research report downloads directly: no form, no email address, no account and no call. The seven study reports below are PDFs on this site, each listed with its date, pages, file size, license, DOI and SHA-256 checksum, so you can confirm that the file you hold is the file we published.

Updated 9 November 2026.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="../assets/print/print-edition.css" media="print">

We chose not to gate our reports. A form would tell us who read them, but it would also stop a buyer, a journalist or a researcher from checking a figure in the minute they have. The underlying data is open too; our [open data catalog](open-data.md) lists every table.

## How to check a file you downloaded

1. Download the [checksum list](assets/research/print/research-report-sha256.txt){: download="research-report-sha256.txt" }. It holds one SHA-256 value per report, recorded 9 November 2026.
2. Compute the checksum of your copy, with `sha256sum <file>` on macOS or Linux, or `certutil -hashfile <file> SHA256` on Windows.
3. Compare the two values. A match means your copy is byte for byte the file published here.

For example, Study 1 has the SHA-256 value `21d03ff5eb70575ca59ad548b2930919bf1aeb3ad5e760621ac307cc73e8e881`.

## What you may do with a report

Reports printed with CC BY 4.0 may be copied, quoted, translated and redistributed, including commercially, with attribution to Broadcastwell. Cite the DOI record rather than the file name; our [citation page](cite.md) gives each record in APA, Chicago, BibTeX and RIS. For a printed copy, our [print edition](print-edition.md) of Study 1 adds a cover sheet with print settings in front of the unchanged report.
