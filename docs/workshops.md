# Which AI visibility workshops can your team take on demand?

Seven AI visibility workshops can be taken on demand, at your own pace and without an account. Each one pairs a published guide with a worksheet, an exercise or a template, and ends with a piece of work your team can keep: a question set, a brief, a fact record or a re-check plan. Every workshop below lists its materials and how to use them without sound or scripts.

Updated 9 November 2026.

To ask for a session led by our team for your company, write to [hello@broadcastwell.com](mailto:hello@broadcastwell.com) with the workshop and your team's questions. We list no scheduled live events.

All materials are free. The guides and worksheets were published on 24 September 2026, the templates on 9 November 2026. Every example in them is fictional.

## 1. Choose the buyer questions worth measuring

You leave with ten agreed buyer questions, written without brand names and grouped by buyer role.

- Read: [Choose ten buyer questions worth investigating](resources/guides/buyer-questions.md)
- Do: the [buyer questions worksheet](resources/worksheets/buyer-questions.md)
- Compare: the [ten-question buyer bank](question-bank.md) from the Manual
- Formats: text guide and worksheet; no video needed

## 2. Read one AI answer and its receipt

You leave able to say what a single answer supports and what it does not.

- Read: [Read an AI answer without overreading it](resources/guides/read-an-answer.md)
- Watch: the "Read a receipt" demonstration, with captions and a [text transcript](https://docs.broadcastwell.com/assets/learning/demos/receipt-transcript.txt)
- Do: the [interactive receipt exercise](resources/demos/receipt.md) and the [worksheet](resources/worksheets/read-an-answer.md)
- Formats: captioned video, text transcript, worksheet; the interactive exercise needs scripts, and the worksheet does not

## 3. Decide what an absence tells you

You leave with a decision for one question where your company was not named: investigate, refine the question or change nothing.

- Read: [Your brand was missing. What does that actually tell you?](resources/guides/missing-brand.md)
- Do: the [missing brand worksheet](resources/worksheets/missing-brand.md)
- Background: [the three measured rates](resources/three-rates.md)
- Formats: text guide and worksheet

## 4. Turn a finding into a brief

You leave with one filled content brief for one buyer question.

- Watch: the "Turn a finding into a work order" demonstration, with captions and a [text transcript](https://docs.broadcastwell.com/assets/learning/demos/work-order-transcript.txt)
- Do: the [work order exercise](resources/demos/work-order.md), then the [content brief template](content-templates/content-brief.md) in Markdown or CSV
- Formats: captioned video, text transcript, downloadable template

## 5. Keep product facts consistent

You leave with a fact record that keeps sources, dates and open questions visible.

- Read: [Keep product facts consistent without inventing certainty](resources/guides/product-facts.md)
- Do: the [product facts worksheet](resources/worksheets/product-facts.md)
- Publish: the [Vendor Facts File](vendor-facts.md) format, with a fictional example
- Formats: text guide, worksheet and a JSON example

## 6. Build a comparison page a buyer can trust

You leave with an outline for a fair comparison page, every fact linked to its source.

- Read: [Build a comparison page a buyer can trust](resources/guides/comparison-page.md)
- Do: the [comparison page worksheet](resources/worksheets/comparison-page.md)
- Formats: text guide and worksheet

## 7. Plan a fair re-check

You leave with a re-check plan: the same questions, engines and method version, and a rule for calling a change.

- Read: [Connect published work to a fair recheck](resources/guides/fair-recheck.md)
- Do: the [fair recheck worksheet](resources/worksheets/fair-recheck.md) and the [content strategy template](content-templates/content-strategy.md)
- See the result: the [completed-case format](cases/format.md), on a fictional sample
- Formats: text guide, worksheet and downloadable template

## Client training: the operating process in four sessions

When a client team starts work with us, these four sessions teach the process our [operating framework](operating-framework.md) sets out, one session per stage. Each session is a set of published pages, so a client team can take it before the first meeting, and each ends with a decision the team records. We added this path on 9 November 2026.

| Session | Framework stage | Read first | The team leaves with | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | The question | Workshop 1 above and [course lesson 1](ai-visibility-course/lesson-1-one-ai-answer.md) | The buyer questions it will approve, without brand names, and the named approver on its side | About 45 minutes |
| B | The measurement | Course lessons [2](ai-visibility-course/lesson-2-repeat-the-question.md) and [3](ai-visibility-course/lesson-3-find-the-base.md) and our [three measured rates](resources/three-rates.md) | Agreement on what counts as valid, failed and excluded before any number arrives | About 40 minutes |
| C | The repair | Workshops 4 and 5 above and [course lesson 4](ai-visibility-course/lesson-4-read-the-sources.md) | The approved facts the repaired page may state, and who signs them off | About 60 minutes |
| D | The re-check | Workshop 7 above and [course lesson 5](ai-visibility-course/lesson-5-recheck-fairly.md) | The comparison it will accept as a fair re-check, written down before the re-check runs | About 40 minutes |

Times are reading estimates for the linked pages plus the exercise; they are not measured. Our [service model](service-model.md) shows which offer includes which stage, so a team can skip a session its offer does not include. Agency teams follow the separate [agency training path](agency-training.md).

## Before you start

The workshops work alone or in order. A team new to measurement can take 1, 2, 3 and 7; a team that already has evidence can start at 4. A team that wants a reading path first can start with our five-lesson [AI visibility course](ai-visibility-course/index.md), and anyone who wants an assessed record can take the [Launchpad certificate](launchpad/certificate.md). The short films in our [webinar library](webinars.md), each 80 seconds or less, cover related topics, and our [RFP guide](rfp-guide.md) turns them into questions for a vendor.
