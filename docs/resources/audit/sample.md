<h2>Executive decision</h2>
<p>Fictional buyer: a small laboratory evaluating formulation-record software. The strongest supported issue is a contradiction between the exercise integration draft and its product sheet. Prepare a bounded clarification of connector type and edition requirements. No observation establishes that this wording caused an absence, that any real engine would respond differently, or that a sale was lost.</p>
<p>Priority order: DEMO-WO-01 fixes the direct integration contradiction; DEMO-WO-02 adds missing prerequisites; DEMO-WO-03 prepares a source-name correction for later authorized review. Implementation and any live review session are separate unless expressly included in the actual engagement. No purchase is justified by these fictional measurements.</p>
<h2>Actual fixture coverage</h2>
<p>Six authored observation slots; three completed; one failed; one untested; one uncertain. Of the completed records, one has a reviewed present mention, one a reviewed absent mention and one awaits classification. The reviewed mention denominator is two. This small synthetic set has no population, independent-buyer or real performance interpretation.</p>
<p>All records use engine fictional-test-surface, interface local-fixture, provider null, method synthetic-example-v1. Locale context: en-US fictional exercise; times are fixture metadata on September 24, 2026. No provider calls ran. The question IDs and receipt IDs are preserved in the exact JSON appendix. This six-slot teaching fixture does not replace any contracted Audit measurement allowance.</p>
<h2>What the evidence supports</h2>
<p>Finding F-01: DEMO-RECEIPT-001 names Northstar Lab and describes connector-based export. That is a present mention, not a recommendation. Separately DEMO-PAGE-01 says native on every plan, while DEMO-FACT-01/02 state third-party connector, Team edition and separate account. This independent conflict supports reviewing the draft wording; it does not establish a ranking cause.</p>
<p>Finding F-02: DEMO-RECEIPT-002 omits the current and former fictional names in a completed reviewed answer about migration. No cause is known. DEMO-PAGE-02 separately omits edition/account prerequisites supplied by DEMO-FACT-02. Missing page detail is distinguished from a false statement.</p>
<p>Finding F-03: DEMO-PAGE-03 uses the former name as current, while DEMO-FACT-03 distinguishes them. A correction draft is supported. No request was sent and no listing changed. Historical records retain their original names.</p>
<h2>Three implementation-ready proposals</h2>
<h2>DEMO-WO-01 ,  Clarify third-party integration scope (P1)</h2>
<p>Direct contradiction could misstate compatibility and edition fit.</p>
<p>Affected fixture URL: https://example.com/demo/integrations/atlas. Evidence: DEMO-PAGE-01, DEMO-FACT-01, DEMO-FACT-02.</p>
<p><strong>Proposed change:</strong> Replace the unsupported draft with: “Export to Atlas Analytics through the third-party BridgeKit connector. Requires Northstar Lab Team edition and a separately configured BridgeKit account. Confirm the documented prerequisites before setup.” Link the approved exercise product sheet; do not state a price or activation time.</p>
<p><strong>Scope:</strong> One integration paragraph and its duplicated on-page summary; no connector implementation or new page allowance.</p>
<p><strong>Dependencies:</strong> Current approved product fact version; Authorized page owner and scoped allowance; Existing release path and preview.</p>
<p><strong>Recommended role:</strong> Product content editor; technical/product reviewer. Real owner, approver and due date remain unassigned.</p>
<p><strong>Acceptance:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Native and every-plan claims removed from the scoped paragraph and its duplicated summary.</li>
<li>Connector type, Team edition and separate account prerequisite visible together.</li>
<li>Source version DEMO-DOC-01 is linked in the implementation record; fictional product reviewer role checks every factual clause.</li>
<li>Keyboard-accessible source link; no overflow at 360, 768 and 1440 pixels.</li>
<li>Fictional status remains in the page preview and export.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Recheck:</strong> After actual publication evidence, product approval and a separately authorized matching question/method recheck.</p>
<p><strong>Rollback:</strong> Restore only the changed paragraph from the owner-recorded previous page revision. Preserve legitimate unrelated changes.</p>
<h2>DEMO-WO-02 ,  State edition and account prerequisites (P2)</h2>
<p>Missing prerequisites can leave an evaluator unable to assess edition fit; no contradictory price is established.</p>
<p>Affected fixture URL: https://example.com/demo/editions. Evidence: DEMO-PAGE-02, DEMO-FACT-02.</p>
<p><strong>Proposed change:</strong> Add below the BridgeKit export description: “Available for Northstar Lab Team edition in this fictional catalogue. A separately configured BridgeKit account is required. Price and setup timing are not specified in this exercise; confirm current terms before deciding.” Preserve all existing price and contract fields.</p>
<p><strong>Scope:</strong> One explanatory block; no price, contract, credit, guarantee or purchased allowance change.</p>
<p><strong>Dependencies:</strong> DEMO-FACT-02; Unchanged current commercial record; Page owner review.</p>
<p><strong>Recommended role:</strong> Product content editor; commercial reviewer. Real owner, approver and due date remain unassigned.</p>
<p><strong>Acceptance:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Edition and account dependency are stated beside export wording.</li>
<li>No added numeric price, discount, guarantee or timing promise.</li>
<li>Unknown price and timing remain unknown; commercial reviewer approval required before real adaptation.</li>
<li>Existing commercial fields preserved in diff; source and synthetic labels retained.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Recheck:</strong> Only after the edition block is publicly accessible and relevant comparable scope is approved.</p>
<p><strong>Rollback:</strong> Revert the bounded explanatory block using the recorded prior page revision.</p>
<h2>DEMO-WO-03 ,  Prepare a current-name correction (P3)</h2>
<p>Independent listing name conflict is supported, but a third-party change depends on its owner.</p>
<p>Affected fixture URL: https://example.com/demo/directory/northstar. Evidence: DEMO-PAGE-03, DEMO-FACT-03.</p>
<p><strong>Proposed change:</strong> Prepare only this correction draft for the authorized source owner: “The current fictional product name is Northstar Lab. Northstar Notes is the former name. Please update the current-name field while retaining historical references where relevant. Supporting exercise evidence: DEMO-DOC-02.” Do not send or alter the external listing.</p>
<p><strong>Scope:</strong> One correction draft; no sending from this lane, paid placement, review campaign or promised acceptance.</p>
<p><strong>Dependencies:</strong> Current-name approval; Verified recipient and authority; Explicit send authorization under the actual project.</p>
<p><strong>Recommended role:</strong> Authorized source-correction owner; product reviewer. Real owner, approver and due date remain unassigned.</p>
<p><strong>Acceptance:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Exact current-name field identified; source supports current and former names.</li>
<li>Draft distinguishes requested update, submission and actual source change.</li>
<li>No invented recipient, impersonation, paid placement or review request.</li>
<li>Authorized sender and recipient remain unconfirmed until the real project owner verifies them.</li>
<li>Historical receipts unchanged; synthetic labels retained.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Recheck:</strong> Only when the actual listing change is verified; a sent request alone is insufficient.</p>
<p><strong>Rollback:</strong> Draft can be withdrawn locally. A submitted request has no assumed reverse action; reconcile with source owner.</p>
