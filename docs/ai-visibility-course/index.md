# What does our free AI visibility course teach?

Our free AI visibility course teaches you to read what AI answers say about a company as evidence: what one answer records, why questions are repeated, what base sits behind a rate, which sources engines cite and how to tell whether a page change helped. It is five short lessons on these pages, sized to read one a day, with no form, account or fee.

Updated 9 November 2026.

Each lesson answers one question, uses real figures from the public Absence Index or the fictional Kalvenor sample, and ends with an exercise you can check yourself. Together they take about 22 minutes to read.

## The five lessons

| Lesson | The question it answers | What you practice | Reading time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [1](lesson-1-one-ai-answer.md) | What does one AI answer tell you? | Separating named, cited and rival events | 4 minutes |
| [2](lesson-2-repeat-the-question.md) | Why ask the same question three times? | Reporting a company named in only some runs | 4 minutes |
| [3](lesson-3-find-the-base.md) | What is the base behind a rate? | Removing failed answers and reading a Wilson interval | 4 minutes |
| [4](lesson-4-read-the-sources.md) | Which sources do AI answers cite? | Sorting cited sites by what you can act on | 5 minutes |
| [5](lesson-5-recheck-fairly.md) | Did a page change help AI answers? | Judging a change against untouched control questions | 5 minutes |

Read them in order: each lesson's exercise uses a habit from the one before. If you only have time for one, read lesson 3: a rate shown without its base is easy to misread, and the lesson shows how to find it.

## Who it is for

- **Marketing and product leads** who receive AI visibility figures and need to know which ones to trust.
- **Agencies** that report AI answers to clients and want a shared vocabulary.
- **Anyone buying measurement**, who can use the lessons as a checklist; our [RFP guide](../rfp-guide.md) turns the same habits into questions for a vendor.

The course assumes no statistics. Where a lesson uses an interval, it shows the counts first.

## What the course does not cover

It does not teach page writing or technical fixes; those live in our [practical guides](../resources.md) and [self-paced workshops](../workshops.md). It makes no promise about rankings or results. For a structured assessment with a certificate of completion, the Launchpad Foundations course in our app has eight lessons and a scored assessment; it is free and separate from this one, and our [Launchpad certificate](../launchpad/certificate.md) page sets out its pass rule and how others can verify the result.
