# GEO Claim Registry: trace the number to its source

**Our 83 source checks distinguish a printed result from a general GEO promise; each entry states its source, setting and verification limit (November 2, 2026).**

Observed answers on stated dates, not a ranking.

GEO (generative engine optimization), AEO and AI search visibility often travel with the same headline numbers. A repeated number may lose its original denominator. We keep the circulating claim beside the original metric and the correction. An unresolved source remains unresolved.

[Download claims.csv](../data/claims.csv) or [claims.json](../data/claims.json). The two files contain the same 83 entries checked on November 2, 2026. This is a bounded claim audit, not a representative survey of research quality or an estimate of how often misinformation circulates. Entry headings state the claim evaluated. If no independently located republication is named, we do not imply a measured circulation history or attribute that exact wording to the original author.

## How do the verdicts work?

- **Supported:** the stated source and setting support the wording.
- **Supported with conditions:** the result needs its original sample, definition or other limit.
- **Overstated:** the wording extends beyond what the source measured.
- **Unsupported:** the inspected evidence does not establish the claim.
- **Unverifiable:** the original, denominator or calculation needed for the claim is not available or resolved.
- **Laundered:** a purported citation traces to no identifiable real source for the asserted finding. This describes the citation chain, not an author’s intent.

## What our own data shows

Our registered analyses describe the September 2026 answer archive and later page observations. Their denominators differ from the supplied-document experiments and vendor cohorts below. The [published report](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23804758) contains the two completed descriptive analyses, a separate waiting protocol and the initial operational crawler snapshot. Registration is not peer review.

**Supports:** the bounded source checks and dated observations specified in each entry. **Suggests:** which source, definition or missing original a reader should inspect next. **Does not support:** a general citation guarantee, a product-quality verdict, an author-intent allegation or a causal effect outside a study’s design.

## Corrections and disputes

A correction keeps the original claim, the new evidence, the date and our resulting verdict together beside its entry. Disputed findings are not silently removed. An unavailable original is a reason to withhold a conclusion, rather than infer a motive. Our current public exports retain the checked date and the unresolved-source limits.

### What does an unverifiable verdict mean?

The original, denominator or calculation needed for the claim is not available or resolved.

### Does a rejected claim prove author intent?

No. A source or inference defect does not by itself establish an author’s intent.
