# How should a software vendor use review platforms responsibly?

**G2 appears in 580 of 18,893 target citation entries: 3.1%, descriptive Wilson 95% interval 2.8% to 3.3% (Broadcastwell, Study 2).**

**580 of 18,893 target citation entries** point to g2.com in the registered September reanalysis: **3.1%**, with a descriptive **95% Wilson interval of 2.8% to 3.3%**. This is a count of archived citation occurrences, not a share of buyers, a rating of review quality or an estimate of the effect of creating a G2 profile. [Broadcastwell, Study 2, top-host table](https://docs.broadcastwell.com/research/data/top-twenty-hosts.csv).

The observation gives a vendor a reason to inspect the platform’s role in its category. It does not justify buying favourable opinions or treating every review page as an independent expert evaluation. Profile accuracy and review integrity remain separate tasks.

## Inspect the actual cited pages

Open the citation receipt and the linked destination. A category list, a comparison page, an individual product profile and a review article do different jobs. The host name alone does not say which product was discussed, whether it was recommended or which statement the link supported.

For your own category, record the question, answer date, engine, visible vendor names and exact citation URL. Check whether your product is named on the destination and whether its details are accurate. Do not claim that a platform caused an answer merely because both the platform and your brand appear in the same receipt.

Repeat observations matter. One saved answer is a dated observation, while a set of repeated questions provides a different base. Preserve failures and exclusions rather than removing them to make a platform appear more consistent.

## Make the profile useful before asking for reviews

Our practical recommendation is to check the product’s exact name, official website, category, supported use cases, deployment conditions and current feature descriptions. Include clear screenshots and factual descriptions where the platform permits them. A profile should help someone determine fit, not merely collect adjectives.

Separate generally available functions from functions that require a particular plan, integration or configuration. Link to current product documentation where possible. Avoid promising compatibility solely because another product in the category offers it.

Keep a record of the source and date for material facts. When a feature changes, review the profile as well as your own site. A technically current homepage and an old third-party profile can expose buyers to contradictory statements.

These are content-maintenance recommendations, rather than a claim that every platform requires the same profile fields or that completing them earns an AI citation.

## Invite experience rather than a particular opinion

Ask actual eligible users to describe their own experience. Use a consistent invitation rule rather than asking only the customers you expect to praise the product. Do not write the review for them, submit it under another identity or ask for a specified star rating.

G2’s current community guidelines prohibit collecting reviews through methods that exclude potentially negative ones, conditioning incentives on positivity and discouraging negative reviews. Its guidance also distinguishes permitted reviewers and conflicts. Read the current rule before launching a collection effort, including any restrictions on assisted review writing. [G2, Community guidelines].

G2 also distinguishes the reviewer’s own subjective experience from independently established product facts. A negative opinion is not automatically a factual error, and a verified identity does not turn that opinion into a representative customer survey. Preserve that distinction when quoting a review in a product profile. [G2, Community guidelines, Objectivity].

## Treat incentives as a disclosed platform-specific rule

Some review services permit qualified incentives that do not depend on the sentiment of a review. That is different from buying a favourable review. Check eligibility, disclosure and the service’s actual programme before offering anything, and respect the reviewer’s organization’s own restrictions.

G2 describes labelling incentivized reviews and prohibits sentiment conditions. Its published policy is a statement of the platform’s rules, not an independent audit of every review. Keep the invitation, any disclosed benefit and the reviewer’s actual words separate when assessing the source. [G2, Community guidelines, incentive rules].

Consult each platform’s current policy before applying a collection practice there. The G2 rules discussed here do not establish Capterra, GetApp, Software Advice or TrustRadius requirements. A shared software category or a citation in the same answer does not make those services’ participation rules interchangeable.

## Respond without pressuring the reviewer

An inaccurate product fact and an unwelcome opinion are different problems. Use the platform’s reporting route for a suspected rule violation. For ordinary criticism, address the factual issue or explain the relevant condition without exposing a customer’s private information.

Do not offer a benefit in exchange for removing a negative review or rewriting it positively. A useful response can acknowledge the reported experience and identify a public correction or documentation page. Protect the reviewer's anonymity.

Review content can help a product team discover unclear documentation or unmet needs. That use does not require claiming that an observed rating is a representative survey of every customer.

## Measure the outcome you intended

Keep profile updates, new review counts, referral visits, qualified enquiries and answer citations as distinct observations. A rise in one does not establish that it caused a rise in another. If you want an effect claim, define the comparison and outcome before examining the result.

The defensible starting claim is narrower: some review-platform pages occur in these saved B2B software answers. Accurate profiles and genuine reviews help readers assess those pages. The study does not show how much citation probability a vendor buys or earns by following any particular profile checklist.

G2 describes its accuracy standard directly: “Product, vendor, and review information must be truthful and verifiable.” This is a participation rule, rather than evidence that a review causes an answer-engine citation. [G2, Community guidelines, “G2’s Trust Framework”; verified 30 October 2026].

## What our own data shows

The exact g2.com host supplies **580/18,893 target citation occurrences, 3.1% (Wilson 95% interval 2.8% to 3.3%)**. The selected-platform table preserves that occurrence base. The registered citation analysis uses the September release, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22907695, and preserves its repeated-link and target/control units. The selected platform rows above are a projection of that released count table, not a new customer survey.

We have no controlled measurement showing that collecting more reviews, completing a profile or using incentives improves a vendor’s AI visibility. The source-class judgments describe publishers; they do not certify every review as truthful or objective.
