# What is agent discovery optimization?

**Agent discovery optimization is the practice of making a product or service discoverable and assessable by an AI agent during a defined task.**

The phrase can refer to a browsing agent finding product facts, a directory exposing a connector, or a system selecting an available action. Those are different discovery environments. A useful definition names the environment before recommending changes.

The library distinguishes [what an assistant can learn from a website](/answers/agents/what-can-an-ai-assistant-learn-from-a-software-website/) from [connecting to a service](/answers/agents/how-does-an-ai-connector-differ-from-browsing-a-website/). This entry concerns the umbrella term, not a universal installation recipe.

## The earliest dated record we verified

Gabriela Marco’s Exista.io working paper carries a **February 2026** dateline and explicitly proposes Agent Discovery Optimization. The author-uploaded full text on ResearchGate records an upload on **6 March 2026**. The document date and the verified upload date should not be silently treated as the same publication event. [Marco, working paper, title page and §4].

The paper offers a conceptual framework and a proposed composite score. It is evidence that the term was used, not proof of a universal agent-selection mechanism or validated performance of the score. We do not repeat its market forecasts or adopt its technical recommendations as operator requirements.

Other practitioners make origin claims. Those claims do not settle priority without dated supporting artifacts. The earliest record located for this definition is therefore a bounded research finding, rather than a sole-inventor verdict.

## Current vendor usage varies

Omnia’s knowledge-base entry uses the phrase for helping agents find, evaluate and choose a brand while researching or acting for a user. The opened article supplies no publication date; we record it as **undated, checked 30 October 2026**, rather than manufacture one. [Omnia, Agent discovery optimization].

A Maven event listing featuring Agent Discoverability co-founder Elliot Garreffa also uses the phrase in his biography, focused on product selection when an agent needs to act. Its advertised session date is **11 November 2026**, which was still in the future when checked. It is a listing, not evidence that the session occurred or that its claims were demonstrated. [Agent Discoverability & Level 250, listing].

These sources share an interest in being found by agents but do not establish interchangeable metrics. A directory-specific discoverability assessment should not be represented as coverage of every browsing assistant.

## What would an observation measure?

Define a task, an eligible candidate set and an observable outcome. Finding a product page, identifying a compatible connector, selecting an action and completing that action are separate events. Preserve failures at each stage instead of counting every technical availability check as a completed task.

State the discovery channel. An installed connector may be available to a particular account while absent from a public directory. A browsable product page may supply facts without exposing any action. Authentication and user authorization can properly limit what the agent may do after discovering a service.

For a repeatable assessment, keep task wording, account conditions, observation date and the expected eligible alternatives. A success rate needs a denominator of attempted eligible tasks. A checklist score needs its chosen weights and rationale. Neither automatically estimates how real buyers distribute their tasks.

## What can a publisher improve responsibly?

Publish accurate product identity, capabilities, exclusions and stable documentation. Make important information accessible in the formats actually supported by the intended discovery channel. If a service offers actions, describe prerequisites and permissions accurately rather than implying that an agent can complete them unaided.

Use current documentation for a particular protocol or directory. A conceptual marketing paper cannot establish that every website needs an agent endpoint or a special root file. A static informational site and an action service have different responsibilities.

Test the actual task after a change. Discovery alone does not demonstrate preference, successful use or a commercial result. Keep each observed stage named so a technical repair does not become an unsupported growth claim.

## What our own data shows

The Absence Index observes **answers to category questions**, not autonomous task completion or connector-directory selection. We publish naming and citation outcomes under that narrower description.

Our crawler study also records requests, not discovery success. Its initial snapshot contained **83 observations after removing our own check, with 0 declared AI user-agent matches; Wilson 95% interval 0% to 4.4%**. The [crawler-log study](/research/studies/ai-crawler-log/) explains the short window, matching limits and later privacy correction. This is no estimate of how often an agent can find or use a product.
