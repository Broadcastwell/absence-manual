# Free academic access to Broadcastwell research data

**Our published release files and derived study tables are available without a fee or form under CC BY 4.0; attribution is the reuse condition.**

Observed answers on stated dates, not a ranking.

You can download the public packages directly. We require no exclusive access arrangement and make no claim that using the data endorses a result. The question set, capture dates, definitions and missing observations remain part of the evidence.

The September archive contains 2,100 target attempts, including five failures, and 150 control attempts. The eligible target-answer base is 2,095. A failed or unavailable observation is not a vendor absence. The August aggregate record does not supply comparable retained attempt rows; the two historical configurations cannot be treated as a controlled transition.

The benchmark records a fixed bank of 140 target questions in 14 categories, 286 checked vendors, five named products and three repeats, captured on September 22 and 23, 2026. Repeats may be dependent. Category and product rates keep their actual valid bases. Unknown search activation, location and historical pass dates stay null. These records do not measure buyer behavior, clicks, revenue, product quality, future answers or causal effects. The [source context](https://index.broadcastwell.com/sources/) and category records remain available. No November release or new version DOI is implied.

OSF preserves the registered plans. The public derived tables are hosted in the report’s versioned Zenodo record and linked from the study pages. A registration is not a peer-review endorsement.

## What our own data shows

The completed work describes the named archive under declared definitions. It does not represent every buyer question or current engine behavior. The crawler snapshot does not establish that a service used a source to produce an answer.

**Supports:** reproducible, source-bounded descriptive counts. **Suggests:** further comparisons whose eligibility and outcomes can be fixed before analysis. **Does not support:** causal publishing effects, permanent vendor ranks, product-quality judgments or a persistence estimate from incompatible releases.

## Additional academic requests

For academic data access or a Claim Registry correction, email research@broadcastwell.com. Additional requests may concern an export not in the public package, a category cut or a question list. Our service standard is a response within five business days once a request reaches our research contact. Attribution is the condition; no fee, form or exclusivity is required. We do not promise that an unavailable historical record can be reconstructed. This page currently provides direct access to the public packages.
